*VP Maintenance Representations/Grievances/Arbitrations/Other News*

I am happy to report that it appears someone with some authority in Metro read my article last month. I have seen several updated requests for change of hours that Metro has approved for exactly what the new language was designed for, Thank You.

We continue to be very busy with investigations, loudermills, grievances, and arbitrations. There are currently over 120 open grievances, with over 60 of them in maintenance.

We are currently working through a settlement on the wrecker outsourcing, more to come on that.

We held two days of arbitration on October 19-20 for the Meagher seniority grievance. We have scheduled a third day in late November to finish testimony, so far this has been very interesting, more to come on that as well.

We have had several questions regarding how contract negotiations are going. Rumor is that we have given the farm away. We have not. We will have a TA to vote on when we have a contract offer that The Union feels is worthy of bringing to the membership for a vote.

## Toys for Tots

The Union will again be coordinating the Annual Toy drive. Keep an eye out at your location for boxes to fill. Pick up at the bases will be roughly December 2nd, late donations will accepted at The Union office through December 4th.
