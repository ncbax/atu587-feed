## Membership Census

In the month of September, we received 60 new active members, 35 active members were deducted, for a net gain of 25 active members over last month's total is 5,024. Our total active membership is now 5,049, an all-time high for Local 587! A year ago, our active membership was 4,736 for a net gain of 313 in the last 12 months. Three years ago, our active membership was 4,157 for a net gain of 892 members in the last 3 years. Our Retiree Membership is now 851, up 28 from last month.
