*Labor Management Relations Committee — to discuss, approve and/or propose resolutions to issues or problems of Metro policies which affect the Union and which either party requests to be placed on the agenda, and issues or problems of contract administration other than formal grievances.*

**Attendees:** Rachael Buchanan, Lisa Nault, Travis Silva, Michael Thornton, Amanda Thames, Collin Dunphy, Brian Eggen

## Follow Up

**Continuing Education Orca:** It looks like the absent person from Metro, who was going to follow up is out sick. It is still being worked on and feedback is really appreciated. Metro understands that it should not replace the knowledge of knowing how to look up the turn-by-turn instructions in the Route Book. Its primary purpose will be for unplanned blockages, and the coordinators will be able to send a reroute to the DDU. Most of the major transit systems use this or a similar technology. Metro is still getting the bumps out and any feedback is appreciated. Operators can do reports in SSaM to report issues.

**Accident Report SSaM Training:** The Union notes that there is some continuing education being carried out, but we do not know what the curriculum is. A lot of time is being used by Shop Stewards assisting people completing SSaM Reports. We have a lot of people who don't want to use a computer, so changes don't really apply to them, but they need a lot of assistance on how to answer questions and how to navigate. Continuing education wouldn't help and at East Base, Stewards help quite a bit. Once in a while, a person from SSQA has been at the base to assist. Metro seeks answers on specific trip ups, and the Union notes that there is weird stuff that happens, and it isn't always consistent. Metro recently did a tabling committee to do with reports. Continuing education is always important. Hopefully we'll have an update next month.

**Shakeup Hiccups:** Immediately after pick ended, the training offices at South Base closed. This created hardships for Operators to turn in route qualifications. On the first day of shakeup with a large number of new Operators at Tukwila Base, training was closed and Operators had a lot of questions. The Window Dispatcher didn't know any answers because it was their first day at the base as well. To date, this Executive Board Officer has yet to see anyone in the Training Office at Tukwila Base. Metro will reach out to training to find out what the staffing levels are. Follow up next month.

**Uniform jackets:** The Union has finally seen one of the new safety jackets available at the Uniform Store and they do not have enough pockets and have no liner, or zipper to attach a liner. Winter is coming. Metro has an obligation to provide appropriate uniform items for Operators who are outside in inclement and cold weather. The Metro representative doesn't have any additional information. Metro would like to remove it from the LMRC and move it to the Uniform Committee. There was discussion on how to expand choices. If there is a pressing issue, you can reach out to the Uniform Committee lead.

**ADA Zones:** There is a new sticker on the bus zone flags. It says, "Limited Lift Space". What does it mean and what are we required to do? Metro representatives don't know, but will reach out to Training. Follow up next month.

Meeting adjourned at 9:24 a.m.
