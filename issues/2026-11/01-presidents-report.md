For True North Values to be taken seriously as anything other than a slogan or lip service, they must include honoring your written and verbal agreements, keeping your promises, and following the law. Examples of the opposite of True North Values would include wage theft or false advertising in job recruitment, both of which would destroy trust and render your words and commitments meaningless going forward.

On October 22, 2026, I issued a Union Bulletin explaining that the County had publicly advertised a $3,000 hiring bonus during multiple operator hiring recruitments after this contractual bonus provision expired on November 1, 2025.

On April 6th 2026, the County informed The Union that it had offered the bonuses in error. Management maintains that those who made the offers were not authorized to do so.

The bonus was payable in two installments: $1,000 upon graduation from training and $2,000 upon completion of probation. We understand that, among those hired or placed in training after November 1, 2025, only 53 members received the $1,000 payment. To our knowledge, none received the 2nd installment of $2,000 for completing probation.

Our Union lawyers firmly believe the County has a binding obligation to pay these hiring bonuses and that the law requires it to honor that obligation. Our Lawyers advised us that our Union does not have the authority to bargain an agreement that would result in any of these bonuses not being paid, since the County owes these new hires a binding obligation.

From day one, we have told the County that they must honor these commitments, and the Union could not and would not agree to the County not paying any of these promised bonuses.

The Union strongly disagrees with characterizing these bonuses as a "gift of public funds." These bonuses are part of the pay owed to our new colleagues.

We have waited since April 6th for the County to state unequivocally whether it would pay these promised bonuses. Yesterday we finally got that answer. The County notified the Union that on 10/23 it would send out 53 $1,000 collection letters. Shame on the County! King County should do the right thing and live up to its often-stated County Values, not treat them like disingenuous slogans. The Union is exploring all options going forward; stay tuned for more info.

## Benefits Bargaining Update/Review

As of 10/26, benefits bargaining is still ongoing, but we could possibly reach a TA on our health benefits before you read this article. Depending on what's in the TA, our ATU International Constitution and General Laws may require a membership vote, which we haven't done for benefits up to now.

If we reach a benefits TA with the County that requires a membership vote, we will hold in-person and Zoom informational meetings with Q&A sessions, along with worksite tabling sessions. We will announce dates and times for all sessions that give members enough time, information, and opportunities to ask questions to allow an informed decision. As a democratic union, we will respect and carry out the membership's decision by ballot.

## Contract Bargaining Update

We continue to meet regularly, making slow, incremental progress on less contentious issues before turning to bargaining, mediation, or arbitration on the major unresolved issues. Bargaining is open, so members can attend to observe and learn about our progress. I will also give verbal updates and answer questions at all Union meetings and whenever I find opportunities to gather a few members for updates. We will not publish contract updates that reveal our strategies or bottom lines to management.

Please don't believe or spread wild rumors that the Union has agreed to, or will agree to, major concessions. That's not true and could divide the Union at a time when unity matters.

If or when the County stalls, attempts to impasse negotiations, or bargains in bad faith, we will respond accordingly. Regardless of how it appears, we are not there yet for either, and we will not risk our union's credibility or integrity by falsely representing how bargaining is going for political expediency.
