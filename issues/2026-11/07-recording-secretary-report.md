## Picks

Pick Season has ended! I can report most picks went without an issue, except for Bus and Rail operator picks. The bus operator pick was the busiest I've ever seen in almost 6-yrs of me working at the Union table during operator picks. What made it busy was……we had 12, yes, TWELVE pages of no-shows during the Full-time operator pick. I cannot stress enough how important it is to be at your own pick, and if this is not possible, or if life is acting a fool, utilize the absentee option we have in place so you CAN be contacted to pick over the phone.

Another issue is during the last week of pick; there's a lot of folks who think their classmate(s) can pick for them without the person indicating this on an absentee. I continually had to explain that it's not that I don't believe the individual, I just cannot go off the word of the person who is saying they're a proxy, and that the person they intend on picking for needed to verify this on an absentee. Another issue was with folks in the same class picking for their absent classmates. The problem with this as I explained to all of them is, someone will now have to forfeit their 20-mins of preview, and that's not fair to either party. In circumstances such as this, I will instruct the person to use their 20-min of preview to pick for themselves, and I also remind them that if they want to help their classmate, tell them the Union will assist them with their pick. And if they're helping someone that's not their classmate, to allow at least 1-hour of pick time between them. To ensure everyone receives their 20-min of preview, I will start looking for their absent classmate, then once they have picked, they are free to help their classmate.

Then there was the Rail Operator issue. What a mess this was. So, apparently, Rail had less work than their workforce. This wasn't realized until the last day, then they added day and night board spots to the bottom of their respective boards. This affected about 4 operators, all who picked the end of the 2nd to last day. They were given board spots in their proper seniority order since the board spots management created during the pick were illegal positions as these positions being added at the 2nd to last day of pick circumvented the seniority of those picking the bottom of their respective boards.

The Streetcar Operator and VM/Track and Row pick went very well. This time, I requested to move the VM/Track and Row 1-hour prior to the Streetcar operator pick, that way I'd have time to conduct the VM/Trac and Row pick and be able to fully conduct the Streetcar Operator pick. This worked great! Rail Service Quality, and LCC picks went well, there was a little confusion with a non-certified seniority list that was sent to them vs the actual certified list, this was cleared up. Streetcar O&M Supervisor pick went well, as did the 1st Line pick.

## Chief Shop Steward/Shop Steward Stuff

The Chief Shop Steward Nominations have concluded. Those who didn't run unopposed, the results are posted on the Union Website, posted on the Union Bulletin Boards at the worksites, and placed in this News Review. Nominations to be placed on the revised Shop Steward have also concluded. If you didn't get a chance to nominate for Shop Steward, you will have a chance during the Spring pick to nominate again.

Shop Steward Nominations for Solid Ground have concluded. At some point, we will want to table to try to get more folks at Solid Ground to nominate for Shop Steward, as Shop Stewards are definitely needed there! Clallam Transit Shop Steward Nominations will be open in December. A bulletin will be sent this month with this announcement. If there is to be a vote, this will be announced in December. And Jefferson Transits Shop Steward Nominations will open in January. I want to thank all of you for your willingness to serve as a Shop Steward! Your hard work, dedication, and willingness to help your coworkers are invaluable! Thank you all!

Lastly, take a look at changes to the voluntary benefits that I've placed in this News Review. BBS will be here to sign everyone up for the new voluntary benefits towards the end of the month into December. Be sure to mark your calendars!

In solidarity!
