## Meeting discussion results

- **CISM** (Critical Incident Stress Management) is currently inactive, and Michael said that he will coordinate with HR to reactivate.
- **South Base Mail Pickup:** Mailboxes are not being emptied daily, causing missed meeting requests and scheduling errors. Michael said he will communicate with those responsible to ensure they collect the mail.
- **Dwell time** for ADA paratransit is managed on a case-by-case basis; if operators feel that there is not enough dwell time, they need to submit a code 10 form so a reevaluation can be completed.
- **Discipline volume** is a major point of contention, with the union reporting a perceived surge in punitive actions. The union highlighted eight disciplinary actions in two days and argued for a shift from punitive to corrective/rehabilitative procedures.
- **Reporting Fear:** Operators are hesitant to fill out reports for fear that camera reviews will lead to disciplinary action.
- **Emergency Code:** Michael says that it has been difficult to come to an agreement on a code word for dispatch to use over the radio.
- **Unsafe Stops:** Stop improvements at Wheaton Way and Fred Meyer are blocked by property owners refusing to cooperate in allowing changes. Operators are encouraged to submit run committee suggestions or close call safety submissions every time they experience problems with stops (e.g., almost mirror clips, not being able to merge to make left turns, or passengers crossing in front of their bus).
- **Over 1000lb including wheelchair issue:** A specific case involving a heavy wheelchair is causing vehicle damage and operator stress; a supervisor has been deployed to evaluate the passenger's weight against the 1,000-pound lift limit.
- **Supervisor Visibility:** There are reports of insufficient "road sups" (supervisors) available, though management attributes this to the onboarding of new hires.
- **Dispatch Radio update:** Updates are delayed due to vendor firewall issues, with completion expected in Q1 2027. Dispatchers are currently hearing both channels simultaneously, leading to missed communications and potentially unfair disciplinary actions.
- **Payroll Access:** Some employees have not received memos or instructions on how to access digital pay stubs or how to receive hard copies.
- **ARC review:** Michael: It was communicated that Fenya's Access to the ARC review files has been denied. Michael will follow up with the team regarding Fenya's lack of access to ARC review files.
