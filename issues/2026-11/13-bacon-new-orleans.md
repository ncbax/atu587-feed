Thank you, local 587 for allowing me to be able to participate in the international Latino caucus in New Orleans. It definitely was a great experience to be able to fellowship amongst the other locals.

Some takeaways were reminders and things that we can continue to implement with voting season right around the corner four letter word if you want your voice heard you better VOTE.

Also, they talked about Cope and how the locals can continue to keep contributing to allow us to be able to have our politics involved our leaders that are outside to be able to continue to advocate for transit service across the board and how important it is to make sure our voices are being heard together

Overall great experience being able to network seeing that the other locals are experiencing some of the same things that were experiencing at our own local

In solidarity, thank you,
Zematra Bacon
