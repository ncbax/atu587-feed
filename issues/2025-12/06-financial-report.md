## Membership Census

In the month of Oct, we received 63 new active members, 37 active members were deducted due to voluntary withdrawal, suspension, death, or retirement. Our total active membership is now 4,762, up 26 from the previous month's figure of 4,736. A year ago, our active membership was **4,471 for a net gain of 291 in the last 12 months**. Three years ago, our active membership was 4,140. Our Retiree Membership is now 807, up 5 from the previous month.
