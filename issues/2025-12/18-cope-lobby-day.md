**You are invited to join your Union Brothers and Sisters for ATU COPE Lobby Day February 10, 2026**

The 2025 Legislative Session will begin on January 12th and will run until March 12th, 2025. Please join us on **February 10th** as we travel to Olympia to learn how our state government works and meet with elected representatives to discuss transportation legislation and funding.

Many legislators in the capital like to hear from their constituents. We will do our best to make in-person appointments with them but will also be flexible to meet the legislator's assistants.

Complete and mail the form below no later than January 30th, 2025, to ATU COPE Lobby Day, 13028 Interurban Ave S., Suite 116 Tukwila, WA 98168 or via County Mail.

If you need to take time off to attend, put your name in the **Lay Off Book** at your work site. Unpaid courtesy detail may be arranged for a limited number of participants. \*\*\***The union office DOES NOT guarantee you will be granted the day off, so please confirm your request with your work site.**

Any questions please contact the COPE Committee at 206-448-8588 or email at cope@atu587.org. ***See you on Lobby Day!***

*The sign-up form (name, e-mail, home address where you're registered to vote — no P.O. boxes — city, zip, legislative district, ID#, worksite, home phone and cell phone) is on page 17 of the PDF.*
