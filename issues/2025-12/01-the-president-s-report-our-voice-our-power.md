You may not like many or any of our local or state politicians. You may not agree with endorsing or donating to Politician’s whose beliefs/agendas don’t align with yours. The cold hard reality though, is that the only way our union can have any influence, or protection is to play the political game. We didn’t create the game or have any control over it; we don’t get to choose who runs. We interview everyone who asks for our endorsement and ask the same type of questions to best gauge where they stand on Public Transit and Unions in general.

Just to be clear, COPE will absolutely endorse a conservative politician if they say the right things about Transit and Unions. We’ve had recent feelers from Conservatives in Olympia who want to explore working more closely with our Union on Public Transit. Conservative Republican

County Council Member Dunn introduced the County Legislation to create the Transit Safety Task Force and continues to support us on vital transit issues.

If you are a 587 Member, your wages and benefits were earned on the backs of the thousands of dues-paying members who came before us. Whatever job you currently have at the County, you wouldn’t be making nearly as much in wages and benefits if we weren’t in a Union, nor would we have the level of public support demonstrated by votes to raise taxes for Public Transit funding.

This was an incredibly challenging election cycle for our Union. Both the County Executive and the Seattle Mayors’ race pitted people we have always supported against each other. Ultimately, our COPE committee recommended endorsing Claudia & Bruce mainly for the length of time they’ve supported and worked with our Union and promoted public transit. But it was a difficult choice, including considering and deciding against dual endorsements.

All along, our Union felt that no matter who won the County Executive and Seattle Mayor races, we would have two leaders our Union has supported and worked well with, who would be champions for Labor and Transit. Obviously, our choices for these two offices lost, and many of our members would have preferred that we endorsed the eventual winners.

I have personally met with and congratulated Girmay twice and Katie once since the Election. They both assured me that they understood our union’s endorsements, that there were no hard feelings, and that they are both ready and looking forward to working with our Union.

I called them both the moment the Union approved endorsing their challengers and checked in with them throughout the Campaign. I was invited to and attended Girmay’s ceremonial swearing-in ceremony.

Our Union is YOU. If you’re not happy with who we endorse or how we decide, throw your name in for consideration to join the COPE committee. Follow the News Review and attend meetings during the election cycle, as the COPE committee's endorsement recommendations are merely recommendations accepted or rejected by the membership at the monthly Union meetings.

If you currently contribute to COPE, thank you for adding to our Union’s power! If you’ve never contributed to COPE, please consider contributing even the minimum $5 per paycheck for the protection of your job, its compensation, and your working conditions. We have many plaques on the walls of our Union Hall recognizing our Local for contributing the most to COPE for a union of our size. We took pride in that recognition; it mattered.

We could literally get back to winning these awards if every member who doesn’t contribute signed up to contribute at least $5 per paycheck. And every member who currently contributes signed up to contribute at least $1 more.

The Union does not speak for members’ political beliefs nor tell them who they should or must vote for. Your Union Dues are never used for political purposes; voluntary donations to COPE by our Members are the only dollars used for politics.

Our endorsements and contributions aren’t deciding factors in who is or isn’t elected. They just give us better access to be heard on the transit issues that matter to us. Historically, we have cultivated politicians who tend to vote pro-transit when we most need pro-transit votes.

Even if you despise politics, or despise the West Coast, King County, or Seattle politics, it absolutely is in your best interest to contribute to COPE. Politicians pick up the phone and listen when we call them directly for help. They draft and vote for pro-transit funding and initiatives.

ATU International’s current slogan is “Our Voice, Our Power!” Our COPE committee, its endorsements, and its contributions are literally ATU 587’s political Voice and Power. Please consider contributing or contributing a little more to the power of our voice, it matters, it makes a demonstrative difference.

ATU 587 – We Are One!
