## In Loving Memory...

You will be missed...Rest Easy

We are saddened to announce the passing of 50-year member, Marlyn Hagemann. Marlyn started their transit career with Seattle Transit in 1968. Marlyn was a transit operator until faced with some medical issues. Due to this condition, they were transferred from Transit Operator to Vault Changer where they spent most of their career working out of North Base until retirement in 1993. Marlyn will be missed by all who knew them. No services have been announced at this time.

Please place these members and their families in your thoughts and hearts

## Weingarten Rights and the Principle of

## Representation

Another important cornerstone of workplace fairness is Weingarten rights, established by the U.S. Supreme Court in 1975. These rights apply in all unionized workplaces and guarantee:

If an employee reasonably believes a meeting may lead to discipline, they have the right to union representation — and the right to a representative who is allowed to participate meaningfully. Weingarten rights are not merely symbolic.

Courts have repeatedly held that a union representative must be permitted to:

• ask clarifying questions • consult privately with the employee • provide support and guidance • prevent intimidation or confusion
