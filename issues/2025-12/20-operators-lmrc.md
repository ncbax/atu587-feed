*Labor Management Relations Committee — to discuss, approve and/or propose resolutions to issues or problems of Metro policies which affects the Union and which either party requests to be placed on the agenda, and issues or problems of contract administration other than formal grievances.*

**Attendees:** Rachael Buchanan, William McCoy, Travis Silva

## Follow Up

**ADA Placards:** Metro pushed it forward to Operations Manager and Rachael spoke with Service Information Chief and we are working on it and elevating it. The language was already approved for the new BEB (Battery Electric Buses) from Gillig going into service soon. Attendees at the meeting are still unsure why there is a hold up on putting these on existing buses, but we are trying to break the logjam.

**Customer Relations Classes:** Training has committed to completing all AVADE and 1st step training by the end of the year. If you take the 8-hour AVADE class, it includes the 1st step training that many Operators missed. They do state there are some challenges and that it won't be possible to complete them on an all-volunteer basis as they are doing now. People may be starting to be detailed instead of relying on volunteers to attend on their days off.

**Uniform Committee:** The relevant people are not present, so the topic will be carried over to next month. The Union noted that the Don's contract is up soon, and it would be in the Operators' interests to know what is going on with the bid process.

**ICE:** If something occurs, Operators are encouraged to call TCC. If something happens that you feel needs to be escalated, please tell your Chief. Going to close this for now.

**Tukwila Base:** Still on target to open March 2026. Initially, there will be 3 routes: 105, 128, and 184. They will operate Monday through Friday Service. Eventually full capacity will see 11 – 12 routes. What if people pick the base, will they travel to another base to get a bus? Metro is unsure of the specifics of staffing Operators at base start up.

## New Business

**Zonar:** Zonar will be new technology to complete the pre-trip inspection. Metro is not looking to implement it until FIFA World Cup is over. There may be some training beforehand, but that is up in the air as this is being pushed to the back burner due to more pressing items. The Union notices that this is a technological change as stated in the Collective Bargaining Agreement 3.2A. Metro stated that Operators will use a tablet that lives on the coach for inspection. This raised immediate red flags to the Union Representative since PED's are not permitted in the yard. Metro stated that it won't be a "personal" device. The Union wants to be transparent about what we're using it for. Zonar can be used in other ways not specifically stated thus far. The Union will refer this to Union Leadership for discussion in the Joint Management discussions. Metro currently doesn't know the extent of the discussion between ATU and Management.

**School Zones:** There was an update posted (Ops Policy 9/24/2025) that school zones are 20mph 24/7 365 days per week. Why the change? Working conditions change? Have run card run times been changed to accommodate the extra time? Are Operators receiving tickets for exceeding 20mph during OWL service? Are there infractions on Weekends and Holidays? Metro looked up the Manual and noted that it seems to have been policy since 2022. Metro's advice is to stick to the letter of the manual. The Union notes that the policy seems excessive. Can we bring this up to senior management for consideration? Metro agreed.

**Encampments:** Ongoing issues in and around bus zones. Most security is not going around the shelters. Customers, Facilities, Operators, etc. Metro requests that Operators please notify TCC and write them up in Origami on a Security Incident Report so we can get more traction on it. The paper documentation allows us to call the local officials to deal with the situations.

**Meeting Times:** Metro has a conflict with a participant not being able to make the current meeting time. Is there an objection to moving the time? Any other day really doesn't work due to RDOs and Collective Bargaining. Can we move to 9am? The Union Representative doesn't see an issue with moving the time while keeping the meeting day as it currently is. Metro will notify well before the change happens.

**Steward Training:** There will be some meetings tomorrow with members being represented who don't speak English as their first language and we want to bring in people who can shadow the Steward, so they can help translate and learn how to be a shop steward. Metro suggested the use of caucuses during the meetings to help the Operator being represented fully understand the situation being addressed by the Chief.

Meeting adjourned at 11:37am.
