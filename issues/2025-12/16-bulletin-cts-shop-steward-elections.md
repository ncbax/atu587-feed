As per Bylaw Article IV, Section 3, the Clallam Transit bargaining unit shall have one Shop Steward for every 10 members, or part thereof and at least one Shop Steward from each Clallam Connect, Maintenance and Fixed Route to be elected for a one-year term in

office. The election will take place in December of each year.

lf you are interested in being a Shop Steward, please send an email expressing your interest to the Recording Secretary at recsec@atu587.org or sign up on the nomination posting on the CTS Union bulletin board. Nominations close Monday, December 8, 2025, at 6:00pm. The list of candidates will be posted by Wednesday, December 10, 2025. If necessary to hold an election, the election date and time will be determined.

If you have any questions, please contact me at recsec@atu587.org or (206) 448-8588.
