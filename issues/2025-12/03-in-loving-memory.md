*"It is not length of life but depth of life" ~ Ralph Waldo Emerson*

We are saddened to announce the passing of 50-year member, **Marlyn Hagemann**. Marlyn started their transit career with Seattle Transit in 1968. Marlyn was a transit operator until faced with some medical issues. Due to this condition, they were transferred from Transit Operator to Vault Changer where they spent most of their career working out of North Base until retirement in 1993. Marlyn will be missed by all who knew them. No services have been announced at this time.

*Please place these members and their families in your thoughts and hearts*

*Please notify the union office of any member's passing so that this information may be shared with the rest of our union family.*
