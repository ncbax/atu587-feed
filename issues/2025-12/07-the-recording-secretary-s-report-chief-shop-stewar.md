So, many of you probably ask the question “why does it take so long to get the Shop Steward Nomination list out”? Well, there’s many factors at play when getting this list together. Just before pick preview, I will get a bulletin out alerting the membership of nominations being open. Nominations typically remain open through the first month of the new shakeup to allow the Supervisors to nominate during their pick. Once that has happened, I will put out Shop Steward list #1. Once members see this list, some may realize they missed the nomination window and email me requesting to be placed on the Shop Steward List. This is where list #2 comes to play, this is the revised list.

Now comes the fun part, Chief Shop Steward Nominations. Now, per our locals’ bylaws, if a member wishes to be Chief Shop Steward at their base and they’re the only ones running for this position, no election will be needed as they’re unopposed. However, if there are multiple members running on a particular base, I will need to conduct a vote. The vote is conducted via operator mail at their respective base. The instructions are to vote for one, sign the ballot, return the ballot to the provided white secrecy envelope, and give it to the window so they can place them in the manilla envelope for me to pick up. If all goes as

By Latrelle Gibson planned, the results are next day. But, if things don’t go as planned, well, this will cause a delay in getting the Shop Steward List out to the bases.

I’m going to be working on a revision to make it clearer on how to vote, and I’ll be sure to send out bulletins to our windows letting them know that I will be conducting a Chief Shop Steward vote and have instructions on what to do with the ballots and who will be picking up the ballots for the window. I will also place reminders in the News Review so everyone will get that reminder, because I truly appreciate all of you who’re willing to step up for the membership and help in any way. This greatly helps us in the Union office when we have that support at the bases.

By the date of this article being published, the list will be out and if timing allows, will also be placed in this very publication for everyone’s convenience.
