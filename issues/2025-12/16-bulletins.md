## CTS Shop Steward Elections (page 14)

*November 20, 2025. To: ATU Local 587 Members at CTS. From: Latrelle Gibson, Recording Secretary.*

As per Bylaw Article IV, Section 3, *the Clallam Transit bargaining unit shall have one Shop Steward for every 10 members, or part thereof and at least one Shop Steward from each Clallam Connect, Maintenance and Fixed Route to be elected for a one-year term in office. The election will take place in December of each year.*

If you are interested in being a Shop Steward, please send an email expressing your interest to the Recording Secretary at recsec@atu587.org or sign up on the nomination posting on the CTS Union bulletin board. **Nominations close Monday, December 8, 2025, at 6:00pm.** The list of candidates will be posted by Wednesday, December 10, 2025. If necessary to hold an election, the election date and time will be determined.

If you have any questions, please contact me at recsec@atu587.org or (206) 448-8588.

## KCM Contract Expiration (page 15)

*November 6, 2025. To: All ATU 587 Members at KCM. From: Greg Woodfill, ATU 587 President.*

The Collective Bargaining Agreement between King County and ATU 587 (The Contract) expired at the end of Oct 31, 2025. Some members have expressed concern that this means that the contract is no longer in effect. Under the Revised Code of Washington (RCW) and decisions by the Washington State Public Employees Relations Commission (PERC) the contract is still in effect until a new agreement comes into effect. ATU 587 and King County are still negotiating and will continue to negotiate.
