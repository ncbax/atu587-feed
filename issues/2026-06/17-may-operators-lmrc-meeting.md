Continuing Education Orca::

The Union doesn’t understand the process for credit card payments. That thing is supposed to be very sensitive and Operators must ensure their wallets and purses are away from the reader. One Operator found out that there was a twenty-dollar ($20.00) processing fee per transaction to have reversed in order to get three dollars ($3.00) back! That sounds ridiculous to Metro. Metro doesn’t believe it’s legal to do that and doesn’t think they can do that. Please get that in writing. Metro states there is no way to reverse the charge on the DDU. Metro refers to the April 1, 2026, bulletin. East Base has noted that information about the fare has been removed from several coaches. We have a brand-new Training Superintendent and Metro will forward this to them. Metro followed up and gave the following information:

• Contactless transactions cannot be reversed on the DDU • Refunds are quickest when conducted within 14 days. If you wait longer, they may have to send you a check which will take longer

• Metro typically charges $10.00 for check refunds, but insist they would never do that for accidentally charged Operators and never charge for simple fare cancellations in the 14-day window.

New Shields:

At night the concern is that you can’t see at night due to the solid black material that blocks the view of the front door and street, and Operators don’t want to have an accident due to not being able to see anything on that right side of the coach. It is an added blind spot at night. In daylight, Operators can see everything very well. Metro wants to report this to SSQA, and you just can’t visibly see anything while making your turn because it’s pitch black. Metro will send an email out and see what we get back.
