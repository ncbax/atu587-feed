A year ago, another L&I complaint filed prompted Metro to put on its best clean-bathroom performance — and sure enough, inspectors saw spotless facilities. With L&I gone, like clockwork, Metro is back to business as usual — unaccountable/noncompliant. How many L&I complaints will need to be filed before Metro is held accountable/compliant?

Washington state law is not ambiguous here. WAC 296- 800-23020 requires employers to provide workers with clean, sanitary, and well-maintained bathrooms. Not “when Facilities start at 7AM” clean. Actually, clean when operators need to use the bathroom.

So: who is going to enforce it? The union? Our Riders might ask, being more concerned about their operator. The Seattle Mayor — the very person who created the Bus Riders Union — might have a thought or two. The answer, dear reader, is yours to decide.

Got an answer or a story of your own? Reach out to your shop steward.
