## Membership Census

In the month of April, we received 44 new active members, 32 active members were deducted due to voluntary withdrawal, suspension, death, or retirement. Our total active membership is now 4,859, an increase of 12 active members over last month's figure of 4,847. A year ago, our active membership was **4,587 for a net gain of 272 in the last 12 months**. Three years ago, our active membership was 4,120 for a net gain of 739 members in the last 3 years. Our Retiree Membership is now 829, up 7 from last month.
