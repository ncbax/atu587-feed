*Why can't we have open honest up-front communication?*

You know the adage it's better to beg forgiveness than ask permission? Maybe TFD should make this their slogan. As most of you know, every month we have LMRC meetings. In fact, we have several of them, all throughout Metro, Bus, Rail, Operations, Maintenance, TFD you name the section we probably have an LMRC meeting.

What is an LMRC you ask? Well the Labor Management Relations Committee language in Bus Facilities reads in part, "The purposes of this committee shall be implementation, discussion and resolution of working conditions, updates to the notebook entitled Policies, Procedures, and Guidelines, issues /problems of METRO policy/procedures which affect Facilities Maintenance, contract clarification issues, issues or problems of contract administration other than formal grievances which are being processed, and other matters of mutual concern."

I think one of the most important pieces of that language is the last six words," and other matters of mutual concern."

Well, rather than bring issues forward for discussion TFD routinely makes announcements at crew meetings on their next big plan or project and how it's going to be done before any discussion is had with The Union. The latest being clean-up of a homeless encampment at a Metro Park-n-Ride. After it was announced to the crew, I had several concerned members call me wanting to know what I knew. The answer, "NOTHING," you know why? TFD management failed to communicate any of this with The Union. Once I learned of this, I reached out to TFD supervision and asked a bunch of questions about Safety, Training and how the work would be assigned. TFD replied and did not fully answer the concerns of the members and The Union. The Union then respectfully requested them to postpone the work until the concerns could be worked through. Rather that delay or have more conversation TFD proceeded to have our 587 Members report to and dismantle several homeless encampments without Police or First Line Supervision presence. It is very frustrating that rather than having an upfront conversation with The Union and working together to make things better TFD continues to do what they want, how they want, when they want, without any mutual collaboration.

## Bus Maintenance

VM leadership has still failed to explain why they have denied most of all the requests for change of hours. Im hoping that at some point someone can explain it to me. And for clarity "Does not meet business needs" is not an acceptable answer. It is like saying "I'm daddy and I say so" to a child!

Holiday unexcused absences update. We met with Metro and a PERC mediator and attempted to resolve the Holiday ULP issue. We could not come to an agreement so we will continue to move through the PERC process in hopes of resolution.

As we get closer to FIFA, I still hold out hope that VM management will start to ramp up OT opportunities and get more coaches on the road and not lose service during FIFA.

## Rail Maintenance

Rail for the most part has been quiet other than a dispute about OT that arose around the word "BASE" in the CBA. I had a manager try to tell me that the platform at the end of the line was a "base." My response was "Are they building a base in Federal Way?" he replied "Yes." Seems like we will have a "base" in Federal Way but, currently we do not.
