The Amalgamated Transit Union (ATU) International Latino Caucus (ILC) Executive Board met in New Orleans, Louisiana (NOLA) on April 15-19, 2026, to finalize decisions for the 35th Annual ATU ILC Conference that will be occurring on September 17–20, 2026.

The ATU ILC Executive Board would like to thank ATU Local 587 officers & membership for supporting the ATU ILC Northwest Representative. I also personally thank everyone for their support.

We have planned an educational and productive conference in NOLA. As the NW Rep, I am pleased that we scheduled the "ReBoot" meeting for the 587 Latino Caucus on May 16, and I am pleased to see the revival has started.

Please contact me if you have any questions about the local or international Caucus, and I will do my best to answer your question or put you in contact with the appropriate person.

My ATU ILC email address is *nwrep@atuilc.org*.
