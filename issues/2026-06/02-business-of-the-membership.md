King County Metro drivers keep your buses running on time. The least Metro could do is keep the toilet paper stocked.

Picture this: it is 6AM Monday at the Burien Transit Center (BTC). You are the bus operator — the same dedicated soul who just safely delivered hundreds of commuters through Seattle traffic — you walk into the end-of-line restroom. What greets you? Let us just say it was not a mint on the pillow – both bathrooms trashed.

You call it in to TCC. You come back on your next trip at 8AM. Still both bathrooms trashed. Tuesday morning, 6AM — both bathrooms are still trashed, but worse — featuring the bonus amenities of zero toilet paper and zero paper towels. You call into the Transit Control Center again — the second day. Response? “Facilities doesn't start until 7AM.” A fun fact for two days – both bathrooms trashed with no warning – “7AM start” when the users work 24x7. Metro believe it or not operators need to use the bathroom – entitled to toilet paper. Who else in Metro is required to endure such restrictions?

“What do I do at the end of the line when I need to take a number two... and there is no toilet paper, Metro?”

Fun Facts: 48+ hours bathrooms sat trashed; 0 rolls of TP when it mattered; 2014 first L&I complaint filed citing Metro; subsequent L&I complaint(s) filed – here we are over a decade later and Metro still out of compliance... HELLO?

Unfortunately, BTC bathrooms are not alone. Rainier & Henderson and who knows where else – Metro apparently conveniently unaware.

Now here is the part that really shines — management’s bathroom? Spic-and-span with air freshener. Apparently clean facilities are available on Metro property. They are just not available to the people actually driving the buses.
