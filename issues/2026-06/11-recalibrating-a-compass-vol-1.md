For many years now you have been a Metro bus driver. During the course of your career you have witnessed many violent incidents from car accidents to physical fights and even shootings. This inadvertently causes mental strain to become overwhelming. You read about an opening at the streetcar. The promise of a cab with a door allowing a safer work environment you decide to apply for it.

Let's now run a hypothetical worst scenario for discussion purposes. After applying you make it in only to experience a workplace environment that is not welcoming. Fueled by hot gossip and rumors. The Rail division behavioral expectations are not being enforced or followed by those that have been there longer. You now have become a bystander in a work environment that is purposely causing metal strains. Again, just a hypothetical scenario.

Unaware of all of this, you’ve now just signed a one-year contract which cannot be broken. Unless through the possibility of medical termination, or a career ending move as the button for the door on the opposite side of the street car platform your serving is opened, leading to a stain on your record causing you to be sent back to the bus division.

What can be proven is not a hypothetical is this one-year

By Vanno Kong contract as a reserve operator for the streetcar. Anything can happen within that oneyear period. Not to have any option for an exit strategy without medical termination or damning a Metro operator career should be considered neglect. Applying empathy, it would be difficult to stomach for any leadership with "True North Values" to justify this as the only alternative.

The discussion that needs to be had is this "one-year contract". Dissolving this one-year contract down to a 6-month contract with the option to extend for another 6 months would be a fair proposal. This easy common sense application change to give metro operators an exit strategy to return to the bus division if they feel the Streetcar work environment is not suitable toward their wellbeing would be a clear remedy.

Moreover, not having to use ATU union resources, Transit disability service for guidance, or have to document incidents to Human Resource would free up a lot of Federal Funding and time for future Metro operators. You will more likely than not have more new operators trapped in this one year contract and the cyclical cycle of wasting Federal funding will... start over again.

The closing discussion is who is going to have the strength, vigor, and empathy to implement the change?
