1. **What does Juneteenth celebrate?**
   (A) The day Texas became a state
   (B) The day enslaved people in Texas were told they were free
   (C) The end of the Civil War
2. **True or False: Texas was the first to enforce the Emancipation Proclamation?**
3. **The last Confederate state to enforce the Emancipation Proclamation was?**
   (A) California
   (B) Ohio
   (C) Texas
4. **The Emancipation Proclamation was signed in**
   (A) 1802
   (B) 1863
   (C) 1865
5. **True or false: Donald Trump signed the Emancipation Proclamation**
6. **Why is June 19 called "Juneteenth"?**
   (A) It marks the day Texas became a state.
   (B) It combines "June" and "nineteenth," the day enslaved people in Texas were told they were free.
   (C) It celebrates the end of the Civil War.
7. **Which amendment to the U.S. Constitution abolished slavery?**
   (A.) 12th Amendment
   (B.) 15th Amendment
   (C.) 13th Amendment
   (D.) 9th Amendment
8. **Which president signed Juneteenth into law as a national holiday?**
   (A.) Abraham Lincoln
   (B.) Joe Biden
   (C.) Barack Obama
   (D.) John F. Kennedy

## Juneteenth Answers (page 21)

1. B) The day enslaved people in Texas were told they were free
2. F) False
3. C) Texas
4. B) 1863
5. F) False
6. B) It combines "June" and "nineteenth," the day enslaved people in Texas were told they were free.
7. C) 13th Amendment
8. B) Joe Biden
