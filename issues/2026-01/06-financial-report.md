In the month of Nov, we received 35 new active members, 25 active members were deducted due to voluntary withdrawal, suspension, death, or retirement. Our total active membership is now 4,774, up 12 from the previous month's figure of 4,736. A year ago, our active membership was **4,497 for a net gain of 277 in the last 12 months**. Our Retiree Membership is now 880.
