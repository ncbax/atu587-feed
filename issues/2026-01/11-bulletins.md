## 2026 JTA Shop Steward Elections (page 8)

*December 15, 2025. To: ATU Local 587 Members at Jefferson Transit. From: Latrelle Gibson, Recording Secretary.*

As per the Bylaws Article IV, Section 2, *the Jefferson Transit bargaining unit shall have one shop steward for every 10 members, or part thereof, to be elected for a one-year term of office. The election will take place in January each year by mail out secret ballot.*

If you are interested in being a Shop Steward, please send an email expressing your interest to the Recording Secretary at recsec@atu587.org or sign up on the nomination posting on the JTA Union bulletin board. **Nominations close Tuesday, January 13, 2026, at 6:00pm.** The list of candidates will be posted by Friday, January 16, 2026. The Election date and time is TBD.

If you have any questions, please contact me at recsec@atu587.org or (206) 448-8588.
