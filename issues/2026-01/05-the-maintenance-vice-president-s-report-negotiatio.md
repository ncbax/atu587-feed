## Negotiations

We have finally passed our protected positions across the table and will get into the true heart of negotiations starting early January, see the scheduled dates elsewhere in the news review

## Toys for Tots

This year was another great success with nearly 2000 toys collected, with North Base Vehicle Maintenance having another amazing year collecting nearly 750 toys.

## Discipline

This month was another busy month, I attended several Loudermill hearings as well as several final determination

By Jeff Stambaugh meetings. Fortunately, this month’s discipline was not as heavy handed as it was the last few months. Hopefully we continue to move forward in a positive corrective manner with discipline as opposed to punitive.

## Bus Facilities

More on the outsourcing that reported on last month. Come to find out, not only did management decide to do what they wanted rather than follow the CBA, TFD decided to call a vendor and outsource both Local 587 work as well as IBEW 77 work. When one of our members attempted to file a grievance, the chief refused to accept the grievance instead wanted to work out the issue without a grievance. When I intervened and attempted to turn in several grievances the chief still did not want to accept them and instead wanted to discuss the timelines, after I explained that he was committing an unfair labor practice by refusing to sign and accept the grievances he changed his mind. I am happy to report that Labor Relations has reached out and as of this writing we have tentative settlement agreement. Hopefully this starts a more positive approach to the way TFD management does business.

## Rail

Last month I reported on the ongoing expansion of rail, and due to this movement and the addition of new employees and new positions, we have had move-ups and will continue to have more as we work through expansion.

I have heard reports that a few select employees have been directed by a chief to check in with their chief or lead before they leave the property at the end of their shift. Im working on gathering the facts but if someone from management could reach out and explain why and where this is coming from because I’m confused at where the CBA or Policy allows this.
