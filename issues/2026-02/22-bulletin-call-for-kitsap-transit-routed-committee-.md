FROM: ATU 587 PRESIDENT GREG WOODFILL RE: CALL FOR KITSAP TRANSIT ROUTED COMMITTEE MEMBERS

As per Bylaw Article VI, Section 1, /t shall be the duty of the President/Business Representative to appoint all committees not appointed for.

As per Kitsap Transit CBA, Article R5, Section 1,

1) Run Committee: There shall be four (4) Union appointed members and the driver of the year, if they desire. This advisory committee will meet monthly.

2) Bid Committee: There shall be four (4) Union appointed members and the driver of the year, if they desire. This advisory committee will meet prior to each service change to advise regarding the draft bid.

3) Equipment Review Committee — There shall be four (4) Employees on the Equipment Review Committee (ERC) to work with the EMPLOYER to make recommendation for operations equipment that routed Employees will use. This committee will include four (4) representatives appointed by the UNION. The ERC will meet as needed at least once per quarter and the meeting minutes will be delivered to each committee member for approval within three (3) days of the meeting and after approval by majority, the minutes will be posted within a week of the meeting.

We ask that if you are interested in serving on one of these committees, please express

your interest by sending an email to staff@atu587.org or steph@binschus.com by

February 6. Please include which committee you would like to be a part of. Thank you for serving your Kitsap Transit membership!
