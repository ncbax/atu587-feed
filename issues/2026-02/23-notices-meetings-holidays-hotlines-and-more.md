## In Loving Memory...

You will be missed...Rest Easy

We are saddened to announce the passing of retired transit operator Vicente “Vince” Caceres #544, on Monday January 5, 2026. Vince was born in 1941 in the Philippine Islands and immigrated with his family to Seattle in 1953 at the age of 12. He graduated from Ballard High School in 1959, served for 6 years in the National Guard, and worked at Boeing for 5 years before starting his transit career with Seattle Transit in 1971. He operated the monorail and drove bus routes out of Mercer Base, Jefferson Base, Ryerson Base, and then finished his career working out of North Base until his retirement in 2003. Vince is survived by his wife of almost 59 years, Leslie. His two children, daughter and North Base operator Kim Stark, son Mike Caceres, Operator/First Line Supervisor/Senior Scheduler. He enjoyed 22 years of retirement, spending his time gardening, golfing, long walks, trips to the casino as well as Disney trips with his family. Vince will be greatly missed by his Family and friends. A Memorial service will be held on February 4th, at 1:00 pm at Floral Hills Funeral home.

It is with great sadness that we announce the passing of transit operator Larry McGhee. Larry started his transit career in July 2001 and spent most of his time working part time out of South base. He will be greatly missed by his loving family, coworkers, and the passengers on his regular work who were happy to have him as their driver. There are no services planned at this time.

We are deeply saddened to announce the sudden passing of transit operator Abdullahi Mohamoud. Abdullahi started his transit career as a part-time operator in November 2022, then promoted to full-time in August 2023. He enjoyed working out of the downtown bases and serving his community. Abdullahi will be greatly missed by his loving father, First Line Supervisor, Osman Said, his loving family and supportive community, and all who had the pleasure of interacting with him. Services for Abdullahi have taken place at the All-Muslim Cemetery on Friday, January 9.

Please place these members and their families in your thoughts and hearts

Please notify the union office of any member’s passing so that this information may be shared with the rest of our union family.

## ATU 587 Oath of Obligation

I,.................................., in the presence of God and the members of this Union, do solemnly promise and pledge (without any reservation or evasion) to support the Constitution and General Laws of this Amalgamated Transit Union. I will keep myself in good standing by paying all dues, fines and assessments required. I will work to promote the best interests of this Union and encourage my fellow workers to become members of the same. I will not take the place of any member of this organization, or any other union worker who may be on strike or locked out. I will not reveal any of the private business to anyone not entitled to know the same.

I will not engage in activity evidencing gross disloyalty nor in conduct otherwise unbecoming a member, and will not knowingly wrong a member or see one wronged if in my power to prevent it. I will not appeal to any legal authority in matters pertaining to this Union until I have exhausted all means of redress provided by its laws. I will be considerate of others in word and action and will never discriminate against a fellow worker on account of race, religion, color, creed, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, age, disability, citizenship, or national origin. I also promise to promote the cause of trade union principles and defend freedom of thought, whether expressed by tongue or pen, with all the power at my command.

I understand there is nothing in this obligation that will in any way interfere with my political and religious rights or that will be in any way inconsistent with my duties arising from any of the relations of life.

This obligation I take upon my honor, and solemnly promise to keep the same, as long as I remain a member of this Union.
