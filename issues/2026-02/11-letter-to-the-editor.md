title: "Letter to the Editor: Stand Up for Our Immigrant Members"
author: Marcelo Albernaz
pages: 8
---
*Dear Editor,*

On December 3rd, 2025, our nation's president referred to Somalians as "garbage."

I understand that our Union tries to stay politically neutral, but I believe we have the obligation to protect our members in every unjust situation. As a country, as a Union, as citizens, as metro employees, we cannot accept disrespect, discrimination, anti-ethical statements from anyone, and this president, or any other, must understand that such statements are not acceptable.

I've met and worked with two metro drivers from Somalia, and both are hardworking colleagues, and as important metro employees as anyone else. Furthermore, at this moment in our nation, any other immigrant might be mistreated by authorities that should be an example instead of bullying honest people.

I'd love to see our Union stand up and condemn this type of behavior. I'd also think we should stand up for the way ICE enforcement's is behaving since the Supreme Court authorized them to 'racially profile' people everywhere, all immigrants who currently work for Metro are now at risk of being stopped and humiliated in the middle of the streets. At least a word of support is necessary and important.

I hope you understand what I'm trying to say.

*Thanks,*
*Marcelo Albernaz*
