## Discipline

This month we continued to be busy with more final determination letters with attached discipline, we have now moved into the grievance process for several of the members who were issued discipline.

## Outsourcing

It seems The Union might need to put on a class to all of Bus and Rail Maintenance what outsourcing means and when to call The Union. As of this writing I have several more reports of Bus Facilities management taking it upon themselves to call vendors to do Local 587 Union work even after several previous griev-

Rail Maintenance management has apparently decided they want to test the limits of outsourcing without Union involvement by outsourcing seat replacement that has previously been done by our 587 members and not to be left out, Bus Maintenance decided to call a vendor to “recover” a coach in a ditch after our members had safety concerns over the recovery, don’t get me wrong I’m all in for doing things safely and correctly, I’m not about sending 587 Union work out the door without an understanding from The Union! ## Grievance Update

Currently there are 58 open grievances in all of Maintenance, these generally fall under one of two main categories, discipline or contract violations. Discipline is usually pretty easy to define, it includes things like, written reminders, suspensions, and terminations. While contract violations are an element in discipline, contract violations are generally defined around issues such as, OT was not assigned per the CBA, pick wasn’t posted properly, promotions didn’t follow the CBA, or ATU work was outsourced. There has been some positive movement from management to resolve some issues in a timely manner that is in the best interest for all involved, there is lots of room for more improvement and better ways to find resolution that works for everyone.

As your elected VP I would rather spend my time finding better ways to improve your working conditions (think contract negotiations), than spending enormous amounts of time and resources fighting over things like outsourcing and heavy-handed discipline. I promised to fight for you and I will, however, Management, You can do better!
