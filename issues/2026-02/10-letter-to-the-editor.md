What I will miss most about Ken is our early morning talks. Both of us would come in before the office opened to focus on certain things before the drudgery of work pulled us away. Always positive, we would talk about our families and run through life goals. When the conversations switched to work, we would talk strategy. He would float ideas of what he wanted to do, and I would layout out the pieces that needed to be on the table. It was jovial, like two kids planning to rob a candy store.
