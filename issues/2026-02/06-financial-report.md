## Membership Census

In the month of Dec, we received 75 new active members, 24 active members were deducted due to voluntary withdrawal, suspension, death, or retirement. Our total active membership is now 4,825, up 51 from the previous month's figure of 4,774. A year ago, our active membership was **4,548 for a net gain of 277 in the last 12 months**. Three years ago, our active membership was 4,098 for a net gain of 727 members in the last 3 years. Our Retiree Membership is now 805, down 4 from the previous month.
