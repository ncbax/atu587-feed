## Change of hours request

As I reported last month Metro Bus VM has made the decision to honor very few change of hour’s request, while most of the denials seemed unjustified without any clear reason, we did have an in-depth discussion at LMRC about the issue. We spoke of a few specific denials, management had a different perspective on what prompted the request, and why it was denied vs my conversation with the requesting employees. In short management claims it’s too difficult to manage a crew that comes in at different times.

As a Lead Mechanic myself I recognize those challenges, however, I was always able to get the crew to work despite the varying start times. It seems Metro management wants to continue to drive down morale and continue to exert authority rather than uphold the True North Values they claim to have. I was promised a more in-depth review of the denials, but I have yet to get it.

## Heavy handed discipline

Both Bus VM and Bus Facilities continue to issue heavy handed discipline and fail to follow provision in the contract that says, “discipline is subject to just cause and that the intent of discipline is corrective, rather than punitive, in nature.”

## Holiday ULP

Metro requested summary judgment to dismiss the ULP filed with PERC. PERC has denied the motion, we now move closer to PERC hearing, and the membership has approved an arbitration request over the issuance of an unexcused absence where the member called in and reported his unavailability 63 minutes prior to his shift. The Union has had discussions at the bargaining table with Metro in an attempt to resolve this, at this point Metro and The Union have been unable to come up with an agreeable solution, we make look to PERC and an arbitrator to help out if we can’t get there.

On a positive note, The Union was able to work a deal with Rail and was successful in returning a Rail Maintenance employee back to work after being terminated. The employee and The Union would like to thank Rail management for being able to work this out.

Part of that discipline is based off of the use of AVL data that The Union believes Metro is using in violation of the MOA, more to come.
