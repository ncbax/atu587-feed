We would like to extend our heartfelt thanks to everyone who came out and joined us for our **Juneteenth Celebration on Saturday, June 13, 2026**. Your presence helped make this event a meaningful and memorable celebration of freedom, culture, history, and community.

A special thank you to **President Penny L. Scott, Vice President Latrelle Gibson, Financial Secretary Marques Blanchard,** and **Recording Secretary Anita James** for their leadership and dedication in organizing and presenting this wonderful event.

We would also like to give a special shout-out to **Cherry D. Gilbert, Michael Mackay,** and **Eric James** for their hard work, support, and commitment in helping bring this vision to life.

A sincere thank you to the **ATU Local 587 Officers** for allowing us to utilize the union hall and providing the space for such a successful and uplifting celebration.

Events like these remind us of the importance of honoring our history, celebrating our achievements, and strengthening our community through unity and fellowship.

Thank you again to everyone who attended, volunteered, and supported this event. We appreciate each and every one of you.

**Thank you, and we look forward to seeing you next year!**

With Appreciation,

**ATU Local 587 Black caucus Juneteenth Celebration Committee**
**June 2026**
