On behalf of the organizers, we would like to extend our sincere gratitude to everyone who attended and participated in our first Multicultural Event on **Sunday, May 17, 2026**. Your presence, support, and enthusiasm helped make this event a wonderful celebration of diversity, unity, and community.

A special thank you to **Abdiwahab Adan** for inspiring and helping launch this multicultural event.

Special recognition goes to **Anita James** for coordinating, organizing, and setting up the event, helping to bring this vision to life.

Thank you to **Greg Woodfill** and **Tamieko Cook** for coming out and lending your support throughout the day.

We are also grateful to **Stephanie Fain** and **Brandon Yambe** for sharing their time, knowledge, and words of encouragement as speakers for the event.

A special shout-out to **Jeremy Une** for helping bring council members to speak and support our celebration.

We would also like to recognize our dedicated setup and cleanup crew, **Eric James** and **Teresa Tobin**, whose hard work behind the scenes helped make the event run smoothly.

Thank you to everyone who contributed in both large and small ways. Together, we celebrated the many cultures that make our community strong and vibrant.

We appreciate your support and look forward to seeing you all again next year!

**With Gratitude,**

**ATU Local 587 Multicultural Event Committee**
