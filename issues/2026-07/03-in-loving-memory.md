*"It is not length of life but depth of life" ~ Ralph Waldo Emerson*

We were saddened to learn of the passing of:

We are saddened to announce the sudden passing of Bellevue Base operator, **Dean Alan Jenne**. Dean was hired as a part-time operator in 2007. He loved being a part-time operator as this gave him time to live his best life. Dean had a personality that would light up a room, although slightly quiet, he would always be upbeat and speak to his fellow coworkers in passing. Dean also received many awards and accolades for his dedication and service. Outside of work, Dean enjoyed his lifelong passion of running, and especially loved running in marathons. Dean is survived by his wife and beloved family. Dean will be greatly missed. Services have not been announced at this time.

It is with great sadness that we announce the passing of retired sheet metal worker, **Michael Larson**. Michael was hired in 1985 in the VM department. He worked in the VM department until his retirement in March 2009. Dean will be greatly missed by his family, friends, and coworkers. No services have been announced at this time.

*Please place these members and their families in your thoughts and hearts*
