## FOLLOW UP:

Continuing Education Orca:

DDU maps are riddled with bugs and Metro states they are still being worked out. Metro will be continuing to monitor that. The Union noted that the reroute map as noted in the video presented by training was not active during an all-day reroute driven by an attending Operator. There was an operations bulletin from May, and if you get off route, the map won’t accurately tell you how to get back on route. Metro didn’t want to add more functionality before they know it works. This system is still in Beta, and if you’re on a reroute, follow the reroute packet. DDU map functionality is only for regular routing at this time. During the testing period, do not follow the map to get back on route; call TCC.

Building a Successful Probationary Program for FTO:

Metro is building some guidelines on this and it is all in draft form and not ready for discussion yet. Mentors are the biggest success on this topic. Training is on top of that as well. Metro is trying to make sure they stay on top of that and want Operators to know they’re human and want the Union to give Metro grace. The numbers that were terminated in probation were very few in relation to the overall numbers hired. Metro thinks the program is going well. Now that we’ve gone back to hiring part-time, is Metro keeping the standards, or will there be something different? Still updating BOPS 108 for probationary employees but should line up for FTO. The discussion came about because we never hired full time operators off the street. If you hear about probationary employees who aren’t being treated right and communicated with, please let Metro know.

Accident Report SSaM:

Bus Bridge:

Line 2 operated a Bus Bridge and contracted a Charter service this weekend. Why did we not do this work? There was supposed to be discussion and the next thing we know, a private contractor showed up and took our work with Federal dollars. They weren’t Sound Transit or County buses. With those bus bridges, ST and Metro have an agreement. Metro claims that at the end of the day, Sound Transit can call a contractor. No legal requirements for them to call us. It was a local agreement established 5 or 6 years ago. 13C and Federal laws forbid using that money to undercut Unions. Metro says it’s not beyond them, but its Sound Transit’s call. ST gets funded a little differently so its not quite apples to apples, but Metro understands the Union’s concerns. Tuesday, our Union President was able to bring the topic up with our State Senators in Washington DC. If Metro isn’t able to operate a bus bridge, and we would like the lines of communication to remain open.
