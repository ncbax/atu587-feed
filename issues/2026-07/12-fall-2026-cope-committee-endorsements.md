Greg Nance, Legislative District 23, position #2: Greg Nance is the incumbent, he represents Northern Kitsap County. He’s an advocate for ferries, schools, affordable childcare, reliable public transportation, and mental health. He has a 100% lifetime score with WSLC (Washington State Labor Council)

Chipalo Street, 37th Senate: Was House District 37, now running for 37th Senate. Advocate for a strong workforce, economy, quality childcare, education, access to housing, and LGBTQIA+. Has a 100% lifetime score with WSLC

David Hackney, Legislative District 11 position #1: Representing the Tukwila and Renton area, he is the incumbent and is on the transportation committee. He has a 99% lifetime voting record with WSLC. Advocate for public safety, healthcare, housing, and education. Recognizes that he owes his personal success to opportunities granted to his grandfather, Geroge, who moved from being an impoverished agricultural worker in the South to a Union member of the Brotherhood of Sleeping Car Porter’s during the Great Depression, and his other Grandfather, John, who left the Jim Crow south during WW2 to join the UAW and make tanks and trucks.

Jamie Hawk, for Supreme Court Position #3: Both Jamie Hawk and Mike Diaz are competitive candidates. Jamie Hawk is the stronger advocate for workers’ rights. In the past, Mike Diaz has represented the Federal Government against immigrant rights violations. We feel this is against our values, so we recommend endorsing Jamie Hawk.
