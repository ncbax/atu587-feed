Some folks wish every bus could be spotless every day, but anyone who’s ever stepped foot in a base knows the truth: you’re already performing miracles with the time and resources you have.

So let me say this plainly, without ceremony or corporate gloss: thank you. Thank you for the work that goes unseen until the moment something goes wrong. Thank you for keeping us safe, for keeping the public safe, and for carrying a burden most people never even realize exists. Without you, we couldn’t do our jobs, and the entire system would collapse before sunrise. It is an honor to work alongside you, even if our paths cross only in passing. You deserve recognition, respect, and genuine gratitude every single day, not just when a crisis hits.
