Hello union siblings, my name is Ross Kirshenbaum, I've been a proud ATU member since 2016.

I wanted to bring your attention to an issue that impacts all of us. For those of you unaware, our pension money is managed by the Washington State Investment Board (WSIB), which is a panel of both elected and appointed members. They determine where our pension money is invested.

As it turns out, over a billion dollars of our money is invested in companies that are actively committing human rights violations and contributing to the destabilization of the social fabric of our society:

Companies like the private prison company, Geo Group, which is profiting from the kidnapping of our immigrant friends and neighbors. Conditions inside GEO Group facilities are so terrible that hundreds of immigrants have been participating in hunger and labor strikes at their facilities across the country, including here in Tacoma.

And the tech company Palantir, which is providing ICE with data tracking software to surveil undocumented immigrants, as well as the cell phones of US citizens.

Or Chevron, which is contributing to climate change, while spreading misinformation about the impacts of greenhouse gases on our health, as well as committing human rights violations across several continents.

And there's also RealPage, which provides rental management companies with an algorithm that predicts the maximum rent management companies can charge. This has been linked to skyrocketing rental and housing prices here in King County.

These are just a few companies the WSIB has our money invested in, but we can say no! There is a broad coalition of unions and human rights organizations here in Washington state called Fund Our Futures (futures-wa.org), which is putting forward legislation calling for responsible investments for our hard-earned pension money. This legislation will demand the WSIB adopt ethical investment guidelines, ensuring that we're not invested in these risky companies who are often paying out millions and millions of dollars in settlements because they don't respect the law in their business practices.

This legislation will protect the value of our pensions and ensure the money we collect when we retire is not blood money.

Several unions have already passed resolutions to support, including WEA, AFTWA, WFSE, UAW and PROTEC17 to name a few.

Let's join the call and take a stand as a union and sign on to support the Washington Responsible Investment Act. I am planning to put forward a resolution that our union supports this legislation, and I hope you will support this resolution and vote to approve it at our next union meeting!

Personally, I believe we deserve better. We work too hard in conditions that are growing harder by the day because of this ongoing divestment in us…the city, the county, the state and the country…they're all divesting from our basic needs as workers.

Let's lead the way and demand investments in a future that we want to see.
