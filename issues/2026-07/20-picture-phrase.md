**Page 19: ATU Picture Phrase** (by Monique Lowe). Fourteen picture-rebus clues, each built from small images joined with plus signs (for example a road plus a King County Metro car, a bus plus a stop sign, a traffic light plus a train, and a bell plus the Seattle skyline). Work out the word or phrase each one spells.
