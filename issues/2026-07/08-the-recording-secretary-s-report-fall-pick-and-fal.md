## Getting Prepared for the BIG DAY, Pick:

It’s time to start thinking about your Fall plans and what you will pick for the Fall/Winter Shakeup.

This will be the LONG shakeup, so this pick will be the pick to really make sure you’re picking the right schedule, route, or base that you really want.

Come prepared with the work you desire on paper so you can easily scratch off what’s been picked and move down your list. If you plan on picking O.T trippers, PLEASE, make sure the work you want to pick will not create an illegal pick due to not having the required 8 hours off or time in between assignments. Prior to your pick date, it’s recommended to research all the work you’re interested in picking and lining this up with the O.T trippers you would want to ensure it will be a legal pick, this would include making sure you have at least a 5 minute buffer time between the off at the base time and sign in time of the O.T tripper of vice versa. Be sure to write these combinations down so you can have an easy reference in the pick room that way, you won’t burn up your 20min of preview time trying to piece this all together.

If you pick via Absentee, please follow the same process to help us ensure that you’re getting what you want and that it won’t create an illegal block.

By Latrelle Gibson

## Shop Steward Nominations

The Spring/Summer Shop Steward List is out, and you will be able to find the list in the July edition of the News Review. The list is also available on the Union Bulletin boards at the bases, and on the Union Website. If you missed the deadline to put in for Shop Steward, no worries, you’ll get a chance to nominate for Shop Steward in July. Please remember, every member will have to submit a nomination every shakeup, so for the Fall/ Winter 2026 Nomination, all will need to nominate themselves during the open nomination period that will start during the July pick preview. The reason for this is due to the shakeup being where some may change bases, decide they want a break from being a Shop Steward, want to nominate for Chief Shop Steward, or nominate for Shop

Steward for the first time. This is not a live list, so please be sure to read the bulletins and/or the News Review for the Nomination dates. Once the Nomination closes, and the list comes out, please let me know ASAP if you don’t see your name on the first SS Nomination list that is posted so I can get you added. Once the second revised SS nomination list comes out, there will be no revisions to this list, and you will have to remember to nominate yourself when nominations open again or send me an email stating you missed the nomination window and I will add you to the list for Spring/Summer nomination window. And with that, thank you to all who are willing to step up and sever the membership, you’re all very much appreciated by this local and those who you represent.

## Not going to be at Pick?

Please fill out an absentee form. You can locate these forms in the following places: The Union Website, the Window, and in the pick room during pick preview. You can return your completed absentee pick form to; The window at your base, emailed to me at recsec@atu587. org, and during pick preview, to the pick room. When pick starts, please turn in absentees to your base window or email them to me, please don’t enter the live pick room to turn in your absentee. Please remember to leave a good contact number where you can be reached, and if you’re going to be out of the country, we can contact you via WhatsApp, just be sure to indicate on the absentee pick form that you will need a call via WhatsApp. If you choose to have a friend pick for you, please make sure they pick no more than 2hrs before or after you pick. For those volunteering to help your classmate, family, or friend pick, please remember that if they pick right ahead or right after you that you will not have your 20min to preview the work, so again, to ensure that you or the person you’re picking for will have the 20min pick preview, don’t agree to pick for someone that picks within a hour or less of your pick. If I see that you don’t have enough time after knowing you’re picking for someone else, I will instruct you to go preview the work for yourself, and I can look up the work they want. Remember, this is why the Union is in the pick room, to ensure everyone who is supposed to be picking, can and will pick. Lastly, please remember, Union picks are not subject to the grievance process, so please be sure if you submit an absentee and are not available by phone that you clearly indicate your work choices.

## Vacation Pick

This pick is for Jan-March vacation periods. If you’re not going to be at pick, please indicate your preferred vacation periods on the absentee form. Please note that the Union does not pick vacations, this is done via the vacation pick room. At the conclusion of picking your work, the vacation pick room will call you to pick your vacation. Please be sure to be available when the vacation room calls.

## Not Listed on the Pick List?

If you’re not on the pick list, this is likely due to being out on medical or other continuous leave during the shake up(s) and you have not returned to work prior to June 2026. You will want to contact Transit Disability Services and/or your Chief to let them know you’re cleared to return to work. For medical, you will want to send your updated medical that clears you to return to work, full duty, no restrictions, to Transit Disability Services prior to your pick date.

## Pick Room Phone (206) 477-0208 FOR EMERGENCIES ONLY

Once pick has started, please note that we’re in a live pick on the pick room floor and will not always be able to answer the phone, therefore, please only call the pick room during live pick hours for emergencies only, e.g., running late for your pick, not going to make it to pick in time. All other callers’ names and phone numbers will be taken for a call back once the live pick ends for the day.

Thank you all for taking the time to read all of this information. I’m here to help and make your pick easier, so please feel free to reach out to me if you need any assistance. Happy Fall Pick!
