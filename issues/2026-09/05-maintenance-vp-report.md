author: Jeff Stambaugh

It has been another busy month, currently The Union has 121 open grievances. We have settled some upcoming arbitrations, voted to advance a few more grievances forward to arbitration, as well The Union has been approached to settle more.

I attended 12 separate grievance hearings, pre-pick meetings for Bus/Rail Facilities Maintenance and Vehicle Maintenance, several LMRC meetings throughout maintenance, several investigatory meetings, a few Loudermills, as well as attending barging sessions for both our CBA and Benefits. As busy as we are I was able to take a long-planned vacation.

Many of the open grievances in maintenance revolve around work jurisdictions. Some of this is management asking you to work out of classification, some of it is our own members taking initiative to get work done. Please do not take work from your Union Siblings by doing work out of your classification. If you have any questions on work jurisdictions please reach out for clarity.

Bus TFD has been a breath of fresh air, UL routes that had been moved up to North Facilities were moved back to South Facilities, some of the 4/10 shifts that had been previously removed were restored and some desirable start times were restored. Thank you TFD for listening to your employees and making things better.

Rail VM has taken seem to have been taking lessons from previous actions of Bus TFD. Do what they want now, don't include The Union and then fight about resolutions rather than making an effort to make things better for their employees.

Discipline throughout Metro still seems to be heavy handed for 587 represented employees, while management seems to run wild with no accountability.
