## Claire Wilson, 30th LD Senate

– Claire is a previously endorsed candidate who has always supported Unions. She has always supported ATU 587.

## Tamara Stramel, 31st LD Senate

– Tamara is a new candidate with a history of community involvement. She lives in an area that is underserved by public transit and would like to see more of it. She is making a good show against an incumbent that has not been supportive of public transit or Union issues.

## Gabe Rtohstein, Seattle City Court

– Gabe is a former Union member and was raised in a Union household. He believes in second chances for offenders, but that second chance comes with a responsibility to do better and accountability towards making a change.

## Noel Merfeld, SW District Court #5

– Noel has spent most of his career working in criminal defense and working with the child welfare system.
