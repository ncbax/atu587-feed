**Nilu Jenks, Seattle Council #5** – A strong candidate for Seattle City Council Position #5. She has repeatedly tried to get an endorsement from 587 after schedules did not work out initially.

**Patrick Stickney, 29th LD Rep** – Patrick knows the importance of public transit and the need for safety because his wife is dependent on public transit to get to work. Both he and his wife are members of organized labor Unions and support labor issues.

**Adam Bernhaum, 24th LD Rep** – Adam is a previously endorsed candidate from the peninsula. During his time in office, he reached out to ATU when there was a legislative topic that he felt involved transit and wanted our opinion before he took action.

**Ashley Fedan, 11th LD Rep**- Ashley is a nurse and a Union member. She believes in transit and spent extra time to come in for a second interview and request a dual endorsement. She is very energetic and promises an open door for our Union.

**Brenda Lykins, Pierce City Council #7** -Brenda is a nurse and a Union member. She is an advocate of safe, affordable transit. She is seeking this position to continue working to make her community better.

**Claire Wilson, 30th LD Senate** – Claire is a previously endorsed candidate who has always supported Unions. She has always supported ATU 587.

**Tamara Stramel, 31st LD Senate** – Tamara is a new candidate with a history of community involvement. She lives in an area that is underserved by public transit and would like to see more of it. She is making a good show against an incumbent that has not been supportive of public transit or Union issues.

**Gabe Rtohstein, Seattle City Court** – Gabe is a former Union member and was raised in a Union household. He believes in second chances for offenders, but that second chance comes with a responsibility to do better and accountability towards making a change.

**Noel Merfeld, SW District Court #5** – Noel has spent most of his career working in criminal defense and working with the child welfare system.
