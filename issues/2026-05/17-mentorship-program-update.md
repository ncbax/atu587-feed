On March 2, 2024 we saw the local launch of the new “Mentors Moving Metro” with the first training class of 40 Mentors held at the Union Hall. The MMM program, then in a pilot stage, was birthed from a presentation to KCM and 587 from ATU International. Our program is one of dozens of partnerships between agencies and ATU Local around the US and Canada. Since then we have trained a total of 155 Mentors with another 20-25 registered for our next training on May 2nd. We have a high retention rate for Mentors but have lost several dozen to them pursuing other opportunities, most often with SIT or SDAs.
