Did you know that mental health is an important part of your overall health? It affects how we think, feel, act, handle stress, sleep, and connect with others. Small daily habits like checking in with someone, practicing gratitude, getting quality sleep, and using slow, deep breathing techniques can help support your mental well-being.

For more information on health and wellness resources visit linktr.ee/kcmetrowellness.
