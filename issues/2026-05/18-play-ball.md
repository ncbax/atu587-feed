Alright, members — grab your peanuts, your crackerjacks, and maybe a jumbo hot dog (no beer, sorry — this is a dry ballpark), because the biggest game in town is already underway. That's right: **Open Contract Bargaining** between ATU 587 and King County Metro is happening twice a week, and your ticket? Absolutely FREE. No scalpers. No nosebleed seats. Just you, your coworkers, and a ringside view of the negotiators swinging for more break time, better pay, a contract that actually works and much, much more for the people behind the wheel, turning a wrench, cleaning, ...

Think of it this way: Metro shows up every bargaining session with its lineup, its stats, and its game plan. Our negotiators step up to the plate with YOUR interests at bat. Now — who do you want in the stands cheering them on? A bunch of empty seats? Or a packed house of fired-up members who are **watching every pitch?**

## Why You Need to Be in the Stands

Here's a little secret they don't put on the scoreboard: when members show up to open bargaining, it changes the game. Management sees a full house and knows this team is united. Our negotiators feel the energy and play harder. And YOU get to see exactly what's happening with your contract — no rumors, no hearsay, no mysterious negotiations leaving you guessing.

Open bargaining means transparency. No back-room deals. No "trust us, we got this." You sit in those seats and you watch the game unfold in real time. That's power, folks. And as we like to say:

> ***"where there are members, there is power."***

## The C.A.T. Advocates Are Headed Your Way!

The Contract Action Team — your very own CAT squad — is prowling the worksite, talking to members one-on-one, answering your questions, and gathering your concerns to bring directly to the bargaining table. They're your fellow ATU 587 members who volunteer to be the connective tissue between YOU and YOUR negotiators.

Every member who attends an open bargaining session scores runs for our side — because every seat filled is a signal: **We are watching. We are organized. We mean business.**

So when a CAT member pulls you aside between runs and says "hey, got a minute?" — give them a minute. Your feedback on break times, pay, scheduling, security/safety - working conditions goes straight into the data that shapes this contract. No suggestion box that nobody reads. Direct. To the table. Like a line drive up the middle.

## What to Bring to the Ballpark – Tukwila ATU 587 office

- ☑ Your curiosity — you'll learn more about your contract in one session than in a year of hallway rumors
- ☑ A friend — drag a coworker along; the more the merrier (and the more powerful)
- ☑ Popcorn — because watching Metro get called out on bad proposals deserves a snack
- ☑ Hot dogs — you've earned them, you drive the whole city around
- 🚫 Beer — sorry, it's a dry game. Hydrate with solidarity instead.
- ☑ Your CAT button — wear it proud. Every button is a statement that we're in this together.

## GAME ON — DON'T MISS YOUR AT-BAT

**Open bargaining sessions are held TWICE A WEEK.** Contact your CAT team for dates, times, and location. **Show up. Sit down. Watch your team play ball.**
