*"It is not length of life but depth of life" ~ Ralph Waldo Emerson*

We were saddened to learn of the passing of:

It is with great sadness that we announce the passing of south Base Operator, **Tyrone Freeman**. Tyrone was born in Youngstown, Ohio and moved to Seattle in 2002. Tyron began his career as a Part-time Transit Operator in 2018, then promoted to Full-time in 2012. When Tyrone wasn't working, he enjoyed fishing, bingo, going to the casino, and fixing cars. Tyrone was also a known to be a big family man, he deeply loved his wife of 27 years, his 18 children, 62 grandchildren, and 13 great-grandchildren. Tyrone will be deeply missed by his loving family. Services were held on Friday, April 17, at the Jaylex Center in Youngstown, OH.

We are saddened to announce the passing of East Base operator, **David Lockridge**. David began his Part time transit career with Metro in 2007. David will be missed by his sister and everyone he touched. Services have already passed

It is with deep sorrow that we announce the passing of retired Revenue Processor, **Paul Pioli**. Paul was a Seattle native, and before coming on at Metro, he served in the United States Army, including time stationed in Korea during the Vietnam War, and later continued to serve through the Washington State National Guard. Paul started his Transit Operator career at Metro in 1973. He later moved into the Revenue Processing Center where he remained until retirement in 2008. Paul had deep roots in Seattle's Italian Community where he held several leadership roles with the Sons of Italy and also ran a catering business, Pioli's Catering, with his wife of 56 years until her passing in 2023. Paul also enjoyed being a mentor, coach, and attending the retiree breakfasts in the South and North Ends as a 50-year member. Paul will be greatly missed by his family and friends. A celebration of life was held April 15. In lieu of flowers, the family requests donations be made to the Tunnel to towers Foundation in Paul's name at www.T2T.org

With heavy hearts, we share that Kitsap Transit Routed Operator, **Norkay Sherpa** has passed. Norkay started his career with Kitsap Transit in 2021 and was very well known to be a dedicated hard work who always brought a positive spirit with him that he shared with everyone he encountered. Norkay will be greatly missed by his loving family and all who had the pleasure of working with him. Services were held April 16 at Acacia Memorial Park and Funeral Home in Bothell, WA.

*Please place these members and their families in your thoughts and hearts*
