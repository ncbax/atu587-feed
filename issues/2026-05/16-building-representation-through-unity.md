title: "Building Representation Through Unity: The Role of the Third Vice President"
author: Abdiwahab Adan
pages: 15
---
The recent passage of the Third Vice President position marks an important and long overdue step forward in strengthening representation within our Union. This change is not just about adding another seat, it is about recognizing voices that have historically felt underrepresented and creating a pathway for meaningful inclusion.

For many years, members at Rail and across our smaller properties have shared similar concerns about representation. These groups play a critical role in the success of our union, yet their perspectives have not always been fully reflected at the leadership level. The creation of this position acknowledges that reality and provides an opportunity to correct it.

What makes this moment especially significant is that whoever is elected to this role, whether from Rail, Kitsap, Clallam, Jefferson and/or Solid Ground, will bring firsthand understanding of these challenges. That ensures that decisions are not made in isolation, but instead are informed by the real conditions, concerns, and priorities of the members they serve. Representation is strongest when it comes from those who have walked in the same shoes.

I want to take a moment to sincerely thank every member who participated in the voting process. Your engagement is what drives progress in our union. Change does not happen without involvement, and this outcome reflects a collective commitment to improving how we move forward together.

At the same time, it is important to recognize that this is not the finish line, it is the foundation. Creating a position is only the first step. The real work lies in how we continue to build trust, strengthen communication, and ensure that every member, regardless of classification or location, feels heard, valued, and represented.

As we move forward, we must remain grounded in a shared principle: our strength comes from unity. In Swahili, the word UMOJA means oneness or unity. It is more than just a word; it is a reminder of what binds us together as union members. When we stand united, we are stronger in our advocacy, more effective in our negotiations, and more resilient in the face of challenges.

The creation of the Third Vice President position is a step in the right direction. Now, it is up to all of us to continue that momentum, working together, staying engaged, and holding ourselves accountable to the values that define our union.

Together, through UMOJA, we move forward.
