April 7, 2025 BULLETIN TO: ATU Local 587 Members at Kitsap Transit FROM: Latrelle Gibson, Recording Secretary RE: Shop Steward Elections

As per Bylaw Article IV, Section 3b, Members nominated for Shop Steward positions in each classification shall be accepted by the nominee. A vote will be held by a mail out secret ballot. The candidates in each classification with the most votes shall serve as their classifications Chief Shop Steward. In the case of a tie, a coin toss will determine the winner, Nominations took place at the April 5, 2025, Union meeting. There will be a mail ballot vote to determine the Chief Shop Steward. Ballots will be mailed out on Monday, April 14, 2025, with a return envelope. Ballots are due back by Tuesday, April 22, 2025, at 4:00 p.m. The results of the election will be posted by Monday, April 28, 2025.
