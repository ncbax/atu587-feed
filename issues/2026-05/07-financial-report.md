Our total active membership is now 4,847, an increase of 4 active members over last month's figure of 4,843. A year ago, our active membership was 4,575 for a net gain of 272 in the last 12 months. Three years ago, our active membership was 4,093 for a net gain of 754 members in the last 3 years. Our Retiree Membership is now 822, up 4 from last month.
