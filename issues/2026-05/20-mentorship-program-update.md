On March 2, 2024 we saw the local launch of the new "*Mentors Moving Metro*" with the first training class of 40 Mentors held at the Union Hall. The MMM program, then in a pilot stage, was birthed from a presentation to KCM and 587 from ATU International. Our program is one of dozens of partnerships between agencies and ATU Local around the US and Canada. Since then we have trained a total of 155 Mentors with another 20-25 registered for our next training on May 2nd. We have a high retention rate for Mentors but have lost several dozen to them pursuing other opportunities, most often with SIT or SDAs.

I am one of two Coordinators. I represent the ATU side and Debbie Fraizer from Employee Services/Apprenticeships and Pathways represents the Metro. Debbie drove for 25 years prior to her 11 years now working in Metro management. We report to a Coordinating Committee with equal numbers from our Union and Metro. In March we reported these numbers: *Since June of 2024 Metro has hired 630 new Full Time (off the street) Operators. Only 33 have been terminated or resigned. 10 probation term. 11 terminated, 12 resigned. That is a 95% retention rate-plus 6 have gone to Rail, 1 SDA, 1 LOA, so total not driving since June of 24 of the 630 is 41.*

I'm happy to say that we have graduated out of the pilot phase and have funding in the Metro biennial budget again in 202/28. In addition to Debbie's position Metro has hired our Ryerson Base Lead Mentor, Tevvis Tillman as our new Project Manager. There is now a multi-year work plan and a draft program manual. Soon there will be a new "Working Committee" appointed which will be able to have more hands-on involvement.

As most everyone will have already heard Metro has returned to hiring new operators starting as Part Time. The role of Mentors will change again back to our approach from two years ago. Our goal is to have 25 Mentors at each base. Last summer I published in the *News Review* these findings from our program survey conducted early in 2025. *"Being involved with the MMM program has positively impacted my work." 76% strongly agree and 14% somewhat agree. On this question, I would recommend the Mentors Moving Metro program to other Mentees/Mentors'. 88.3% strongly agree.*

I want to thank the support of President Greg Woodfill and our Full Time Officers and the Metro Leadership. As we continue to grow and mature we see the value of replicating our program into other operational divisions. Working with the International Staff has been a great part of this partnership along with my day-to-day work with Debbie. BUT—my greatest appreciation goes out to our Mentors and Lead Mentors. These are truly "pay it forward" Operators who live up to the highest standards of professionalism. I wish I could name each one of them in this article, but sometime soon I will publish their names. I thank everyone on our MMM team, Management and Union, for truly making a difference.
