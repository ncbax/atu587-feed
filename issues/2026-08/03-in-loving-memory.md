We were saddened to learn of the passing of:

It's with great sadness that we announce the passing of retired transit operator, **Michael Kelley** #1276 was born September 10, 1949, in Salt Lake City, Utah. His family moved him and his 3 siblings to Bainbrige Island where he grew up. Shortly after graduating from Bainbridge Island High School in 1968, he enlisted in the United States Air Force where he remained until honorably discharged in 1974. Once back in Seattle, he got a job at Boeing where he worked as a mechanic for several years before coming to Metro in 1976. He remained a transit operator primarily working out of South Base until his retirement in 2006. I retirement life, he enjoyed traveling, cars, carpentry, and his family. He will be greatly missed by his loving siblings and extended family. Services will be held at Corbeill Funeral Home in Colfax at a later date.
