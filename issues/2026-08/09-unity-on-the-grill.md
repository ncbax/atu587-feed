This summer, I came together with our union family for a cookout that reminded me why we stand strong as one. Tasty food, good company, and the kind of camaraderie that only comes from a group that truly looks out for each other; that's what the Summer Union Cookout 2026 was all about.

As you know, none of it happens without the people who give their time behind the scenes, so we want to take a moment to say thank you.

Thank you, **Paul Neil** and **Lisa Nault** for orchestrating the entire event from start to finish. Your planning and coordination turned an idea into a day the membership will remember. Thank you to **Greg Woodfill and Rachael Buchanan** for taking the great photos for the *News Review*.

Thank you, **Michelle Penny, Anita James, Mary Prather, Rachael Buchanan, Greg Woodfill, Cory Rigtrup, and others**, for volunteering to help serve our hungry members, and thank you, **Rachael Buchanan, Tonya Abernathy, Greg Woodfill, Cory Rigtrup, Latrelle Gibson, Brian St. Pierre, Robert Rothwell, Stan Straker, and Karl Niedermann,** for volunteering to help with the task of set up, clean up, and everything in between.

Thank you, **Felicia Hurt and Tonya Abernathy** for volunteering to oversee the membership roster and hand out food and prize tickets to attendees, to **Zematra Bacon** for volunteering to run the bingo table, and to **Abdiwahab Adan** for bringing the sambusa's.

Thank you, **Jeff Stambaugh** and **Jerry McKenna** for volunteering to work the grill all day and making sure everyone left with a full plate and a smile.

Thank you, **Bill Clifford** for keeping spirits and cones high, handing out ice cream to members all afternoon.

And to **Dorene Kelly, Jeff Cushnyr, Stephanie Binschus, Fenya Joy,** and many, many more who lent a hand in ways big and small, thank you.

And to every member who came out, ate, laughed, and spent the afternoon with fellow union sisters, brothers, and siblings, thank you for showing up. A cookout is only as good as the people who fill it, and your presence is what made this day special.

We know that this is the spirit that makes our union strong: showing up for each other, whether it's at the bargaining table or around the grill.

Thank you to everyone named above, and to all who helped make Summer Union Cookout 2026 a day worth remembering. And thank you to all of those who may not be mentioned above for pitching in wherever needed and keeping everything running smoothly, from set up to cleanup.

Here's to more moments like this, together.
