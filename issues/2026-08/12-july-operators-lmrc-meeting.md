The Union heard that Metro is going to require Operators to take the train to road reliefs in the fall shakeup and will no longer be given cars. Is this actually a thing that is going to start happening? The Planners make that call and they’re ATU, so ask them. The Union has sent a demand to bargain over this, and nobody has said anything. Are the Base Planners responsible for making the decision to decide who gets a car and who takes the train? The Run Cards do not indicate whether you take the train or a car.
