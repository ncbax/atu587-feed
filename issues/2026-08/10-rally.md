On June 28, 2026, Seattle held the Annual Pride Parade in Downtown Seattle. ATU 587 showed up and marched alongside the County in a festive display of Joy by everyone marching and watching! The theme of this year's parade was, "Rally!"

The ATU 587 Local Pride Caucus was proud to march alongside the ATU Local 587 Black Caucus, ATU Local 587 Women's Caucus, and ATU Local 758 Transit Togetherness Pride Caucus from Tacoma.

Erice James Operated the Pride Bus through the parade and was exceptional in that role! Jo Gries trained on the Gillig BEB that operates out of Tukwila Base and drove that coach during the Parade!

ATU Local 587 Black Caucus President, Penny L Scott kept the crowd going with loud and proud chants through the megaphone, while the crowd chanted, "Gay Bus! Gay Bus!"

Our Local Full-Time Officers not attending the Northwest Conference were also in attendance to include, VP Cory Rigtrup, VP2, Jeff Stambaugh, and Financial Secretary, Paul Neil. Thank you for supporting our Caucus!

Thank you all for attending and we look forward to seeing you in our monthly meetings as we plan to host the 3rd Annual International Pride Caucus Conference in Seattle, March 18-20th, 2027!
