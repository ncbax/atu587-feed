Pick time is right around the corner, well, for the Rail folks, you just had a repick in January, but for Streetcar, it's been a good minute since the Fall 2025 pick. The absentees should be at the bases, Rail and Streetcar driver timecards, the run cuts, and the Seniority list should be out by the time this article is published. The driver timecards for both Rail and Streetcar should be on the website as well, those are usually up prior to the seniority list.

## Pick Dates

Rail Operator Pick will be conducted March 9 – 11 and Streetcar pick will be conducted March 13.

## Vacation Pick

This is the vacation must pick for Rail. If you're filling out an absentee, please be sure to indicate the vacation periods you want to pick on your absentee. For reference, please log onto the Union Website to view the 2026 vacation periods. Just another note, this is not vacation pick for Streetcar, that will happen during the Fall pick.

## Pick Process

For Rail, there will be a pick preview outside of the pick room. The work is generally updated in live time, so you'll know what is left to pick prior to entering the pick room. For absentee picks, please be sure to give us a good working phone number that you will be able to be reached at. For Streetcar, you can view the work 20 min prior to your pick time. If you receive a call from an unknown number, please answer as this may be us trying to contact you for your pick. Remember, if you miss your pick, the Union will have to pick for you, and Union picks cannot be grieved.

Happy pick and see you there!
