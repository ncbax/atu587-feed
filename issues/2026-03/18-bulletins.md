## Shop Steward Elections for Spring/Summer 2026 Shakeup (King County Metro)

*Bulletin dated February 17, 2026. To: All Local 587 Members at KCM. From: Latrelle Gibson, Recording Secretary.*

Pursuant to the Bylaws and Rules of Order of Local 587, Article IV, Section 6, Paragraphs (a), (b), (c), and (d), this notice serves to open nominations for Shop Stewards. Nominations must be submitted in writing to the Union office no later than **Friday, April 10, 2026**. Any members in good standing interested in being a Shop Steward must nominate themselves. Nomination forms will be available at the worksites, on the ATU 587 website or E-mail nominations are accepted by sending an email to the Recording Secretary, Latrelle Gibson recsec@atu587.org.

Anyone interested in serving as Chief Shop Steward should submit their request in writing at the same time, although nominations will be accepted for the Chief Shop Steward position for a period of five (5) days past the election of the Shop Stewards.

## WPSL Changes & Explanation (Kitsap Transit)

*Bulletin dated February 11, 2026. To: All ATU 587 Members at Kitsap Transit. From: Latrelle Gibson, Recording Secretary.*

We are hearing that there is still some confusion over the upcoming changes to WPSL. All Full-Time and Extra-Board Operators will receive 20 hours of GL. If you are **Part-time,** you will NOT receive these hours.

Please note that your GL Bank will determine the number of hours designated as WPSL.

If your GL bank has 80 hours after the additional 20 hours are added, 40 of those hours will be designated as WPSL-qualified. If you took 2 weeks (10 days at 8 hours) without working additional hours, you would eliminate all your GL and WPSL designated hours.

If your GL bank has 40 hours after the additional 20 hours have been added, all 40 hours will be designated as WPSL. Please note that if your GL bank is empty, you will receive the 20 hours of GL and have 20 hours of WPSL designated.

*A bar chart (in hours) illustrates the three cases: 80 Total, 40 Total, and 20 WPSL.*

## Executive Board Vehicle Maintenance Position #3 Vacancy

*Bulletin dated February 17, 2026. To: All ATU Local 587 Vehicle Maintenance Members. From: Latrelle Gibson, Recording Secretary.*

At this time, due to Cory Rigtrup being appointed to Vice President #1, the Executive Board position #3 that represents Vehicle Maintenance is now vacant.

Pursuant to Article III, Section 2 (i), this vacancy will be filled by a majority vote of the remaining Executive Board members. All Vehicle Maintenance members wishing to apply for this position must submit a letter of interest to the Executive Board for consideration. **A letter of interest may be emailed to the recording secretary recsec@atu587.org or sent to the Union office. These letters of interest must arrive no later than Monday, March 23, 2026, by 12:00 p.m.** Any applicant wishing to **appear before the Executive Board must state so in their letter** and be available to address the Executive Board between the hours of 9:00 a.m. and 12:00 p.m. on Tuesday, March 24, 2026.

This vacancy will be filled at the Executive Board meeting of March 24, 2026. Installation of the new officer will take place at the following Charter Membership meeting on April 6, 2026.
