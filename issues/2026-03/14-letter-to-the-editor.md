Dear Editor,

Opinion: Vote NO on a 3rd Vice President. Let’s spend our money on members, not more full-time union officers

Our union is asking members to vote on a big change: adding a third Vice President (VP).

I have been a shop steward for over 26 years. I care deeply about our union and our members.

In my opinion, adding a third VP is the wrong choice. I am asking you to Vote NO.

Why I say NO 1. It costs too much A new Vice President will cost about $180,000 every year.

This money comes from your union dues.

This new VP would mainly represent only about 20% of the members.

It is not right to use everyone’s money for a position that helps only a small group.

2. Use the leaders we already have

We have already elected Executive Board members to represent us at our bases and properties.

They were elected to help members with problems and grievances.

Before we spend money on another top position, we should fully use the leaders we have already elected.

They should represent all members at their assigned bases.

That is what we elected them to do.

3. A better idea: a Union

Lawyer

If we want to spend money to help members, there is a better way.

We should hire a full-time Union Lawyer —a lawyer who works only for our union.

A Union Lawyer would: • Know our contracts very well

• Fight King County and other employers faster

• Work only for our members, not outside clients

• Help us get better results in bargaining and disputes

Other unions do this, and it works.

This is a smarter and stronger use of our dues.

The bottom line A third Vice President costs a lot of money and does not fix our real problems.

It does not help the whole membership.

Please Vote NO on the 3rd Vice President.

Let’s spend our money in a way that helps all members.

Let’s build a stronger union by using our dues wisely.

Tzur Wilfand, Bellevue Base 26+ Years as Shop-Steward.
