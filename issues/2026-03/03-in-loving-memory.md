We were saddened to learn of the passing of:

It is with deep sadness that we announce the passing of retired Central Base operator, **Mary Hopson**. Mary started her transit career in 1980 and in that time, earned a 37-year safe driver award, and in 2019, earned Operator of the Year. Mary enjoyed driving routes out of Downtown and was a well-known figure on the route 106 that ran out of Central Base. Mary retired from Metro in June 2019 and enjoyed traveling and spending time with her family. Mary was born and raised in Seattle and will be greatly missed by her loving family, extended family, friends, and the community that she served on her route 106 that has grown to love and appreciate her for her loving spirit and compassion. Mary is survived by her husband of 42 years, two children, and five grandchildren.

*Please place these members and their families in your thoughts and hearts*
