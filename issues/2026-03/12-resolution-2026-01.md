**A RESOLUTION AFFIRMING SOLIDARITY, EQUALITY, AND MUTUAL RESPECT AND CONDEMNING RACISM, ISLAMOPHOBIA, XENOPHOBIA, AND ATTACKS ON THE LGBTQIA+ COMMUNITY AGAINST MEMBERS AND COMMUNITIES REPRESENTED BY ATU LOCAL 587**

**WHEREAS,** the Amalgamated Transit Union was established on the principles of unity, solidarity, and mutual aid among working people, and the ATU International Constitution affirms the equal dignity, rights, and protections of all members without distinction as to race, color, creed, religion, national origin, gender, gender expression, LGBTQIA+ community, or background; and

**WHEREAS,** ATU Local 587 represents a diverse membership that includes immigrants, refugees, asylum seekers, naturalized citizens, LGBTQIA+ people, and U.S.-born workers who collectively operate and maintain public transportation systems relied upon by the communities we serve; and

**WHEREAS,** a substantial portion of ATU Local 587 members work in public-facing and safety-sensitive positions, including bus and rail operations, supervision, and frontline service, where they are highly visible and frequently exposed to public interaction; and

**WHEREAS,** racism, Islamophobia, and xenophobia, including attacks on immigrant and refugee communities based on national origin, culture, language, religion, or perceived immigration status, LGBTQIA+ status undermine social cohesion and directly threaten the safety, dignity, and well-being of transit workers; and

**WHEREAS,** xenophobia and homophobia often manifests through collective blame and dehumanization of entire communities and does not distinguish between citizenship, immigration status, or legal standing, thereby placing all members associated with targeted communities at risk; and

**WHEREAS,** rhetoric originating from public officials or positions of authority carries significant influence and can normalize discriminatory behavior, embolden harassment, and create hostile conditions for transit workers who cannot disengage from the public while performing their duties; and

**WHEREAS,** Muslim members, particularly women who wear hijab or other religious attire, as well as immigrant members from historically targeted or war-torn regions, and the LGBTQIA+ communities experience heightened vulnerability during periods of increased fear-mongering and xenophobic and homophobic and transphobic rhetoric; and

**WHEREAS,** many members of ATU Local 587 have personal or family histories shaped by war, displacement, or political instability, and membership in the LGBTQIA+ communities, and are acutely aware of the real-world consequences of dehumanizing language and divisive rhetoric; and

**WHEREAS,** ATU Local 587 acknowledges that racism, Islamophobia, and xenophobia, homophobia and transphobia can also appear within workplaces and union environments through casual remarks, stereotypes, jokes, or dismissive language that demean coworkers based on culture, accent, religion, gender, gender identity and expression, or national origin; and

**WHEREAS,** such conduct, whether intentional or casual, erodes trust, damages morale, and weakens the solidarity upon which the labor movement and this Union depend; and

**WHEREAS,** the ATU International Constitution places responsibility on all members to uphold the honor and integrity of the Union and to refrain from actions or language that bring harm, division, or discredit upon fellow members or the Union as a whole; and

**WHEREAS,** true solidarity requires not only collective defense against external threats to members, but also accountability and respect within our own membership;

**THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED,** that ATU Local 587 unequivocally condemns racism, Islamophobia, xenophobia, homophobia, and transphobia, including attacks on immigrant, refugee, and minority communities, whether expressed by public officials, institutions, or individuals; and

**BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED,** that ATU Local 587 reaffirms its commitment to ensuring that every member, regardless of race, religion, national origin, immigration status, language, LGBTQIA+ status, or cultural background, is treated with dignity, respect, and fairness in the workplace and within the Union; and

**BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED,** that ATU Local 587 calls upon all officers, representatives, stewards, and members to actively uphold these principles in their conduct, language, and actions and to challenge behavior that undermines unity or creates a hostile or unsafe environment; and

**BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED,** that ATU Local 587 affirms solidarity with Muslim members, immigrant members, refugee members, and all members whose communities have been targeted by xenophobia, homophobia, transphobia and supports their right to work and participate in union life free from harassment, intimidation, or discrimination; and

**BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED,** that ATU Local 587 encourages education, awareness, and dialogue consistent with union values to strengthen understanding, reduce harm, and reinforce solidarity among members; and

**BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED,** that ATU Local 587 declares that racism, Islamophobia, xenophobia, homophobia, transphobia, and all forms of dehumanization have no place in this Union, and that unity, respect, and solidarity remain foundational to the mission and strength of the Amalgamated Transit Union.
