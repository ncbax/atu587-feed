## Discipline

The seemingly never-ending cycle of discipline continues. There have been several more investigatory meetings as well as Loudermill hearings around work performance and "time theft". Metro claims employees are not working during work hours and claiming pay for such hours or employees are not on the property during work hours. Please do not put yourself in this position.

## Outsourcing

Several more grievances have been filed over outsourcing of Local 587 work, somehow management still hasn't figured out the language.

## Grievances

Currently I have 59 open grievances, while a few of these have been resolved at a low level, I truly don't understand why others have progressed so far into the process rather than finding a resolution somewhere along the way. Sometimes I think Metro is just attempting to frustrate The Union and trying to keep us busy.

## Bus Facilities Maintenance

Does it ever end?

Apparently not.

As most of you recall in 2024 TFD management proposed pick positions outside the Collective bargaining agreement, The Union filed an unfair labor practice and MOA's were written and agreed on how to move forward. Those agreements expired Dec 31,2025. Prior to the expiration of those agreements, The Union put TFD management on notice those agreements were going to expire. TFD failed to address the expired MOA's. Now TFD has once again proposed pick positions outside of the language of the Collective Bargaining Agreement based on expired MOA's.

At the pre-pick meeting, The Union requested that the pick positions be updated to reflect the language in the collective bargaining agreement, TFD management so far has refused.

TFD management has tried to insinuate that The Union has held up the pick, that is not true, The Union has instructed management to post and conduct the pick in accordance with the Collective Bargaining Agreement.
