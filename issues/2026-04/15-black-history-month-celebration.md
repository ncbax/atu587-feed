The Recording Secretary would like to thank you all for coming out and celebrating black history month with us, it was great to commune with all of you and reflect on what this month means to us and how we got here! I look forward to seeing all of you at the multicultural event in May. The exact date and time are included in this edition of the News Review, as well as on the Union website. And a special thanks to James Reese for taking these great photos. A thank you goes out to Anita James, Esther Hankserson, Zematra Bacon, Monque Lowe, and Tia Spellman for helping with food service, and a thank you to Anita James, Esther Hankerson, Monque Lowe, Mechelle Penny, and Marques Blanchard for helping with the cleanup.

Thank you all!

In solidarity, Latrelle Gibson
