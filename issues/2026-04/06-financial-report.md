## Membership Census

In the month of Feb, we received 50 new active members, 32 active members were deducted due to voluntary withdrawal, suspension, death, or retirement. Our total active membership is now 4,843, an increase of 18 active members over last month's figure of 4,825. A year ago, our active membership was 4,574 for a net gain of 269 in the last 12 months. Three years ago, our active membership was 4,103 for a net gain of 740 members in the last 3 years. Our Retiree Membership is now 818, down 2 from last month.
