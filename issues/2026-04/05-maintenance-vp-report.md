## Bus Facilities Maintenance

Will the drama ever end? Apparently not. TFD management continues to erode seniority and destroy employee morale. As of this writing management has not posted or conducted the pick, this is now affecting lives. Our members and their employees have things to plan, like vacations and childcare, only they can't because TFD management has failed to do their job. As I reported last month, they have proposed positions outside the collective bargaining agreement. The more I learn the more upsetting it gets. One of the largest classifications in TFD is called Utility Laborer, their main job is to clean the Bus Zones and make them presentable to our customers. In 2019 there were 43 pick positions for this job classification, 6 of these were marked "Usually shelter maintenance as assigned--see supplemental". Basically, what that meant was you came into work and were assigned that day's work based on need for the day, similar to a report operator. For the most part the other 37 picked positions had known work to pick, basically you knew what you were going to do nearly every day, part of knowing your picked work was a route sheet. The route sheet (like a run card) spelled out the location of bus stops you would clean and how often you would clean them. Now, the proposed pick sheets have 46 pick positions with 27, yes you read that correct, 27 "Usually shelter maintenance as assigned--see supplemental". Why would you need 27 of these you ask? Well, I did and TFD management gave me a song and dance about the ability to get the work done and lack of equipment to do it. The reality? The route sheets haven't been updated in over a decade, and management doesn't seem to be able to figure out how get the work done in a timely, consistent, cost-effective manner. Instead, they want to be able to direct the workforce to run around and service the stops that are not being serviced frequently enough because the route sheets haven't been updated. Would operations expect over 50% of the workforce to come into work and be assigned work daily?

The Union has made management a proposal that in our opinion will both get the work done and restore seniority. Management's response? That doesn't work for us.

Hopefully by the time you read this we have reached an agreement that works for everyone.

## Bus Vehicle Maintenance

As most of you are aware we just concluded the Spring VM pick, it was going smooth until the final day when management announced Superintendent promotions and location changes, right behind that announcement the chiefs had a move-up with considerable movement causing frustration that employees could now be working with a chief or superintendent they intentionally avoided during the pick. The Union immediately asked for a repick and was denied. Hopefully in the future we can find a way to get management movement announced prior to the pick or find a workable solution that doesn't leave employees upset.

Rail Maintenance was fairly quiet last month, Hopefully that is good sign.
