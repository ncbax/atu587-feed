## Chief Shop Steward/Shop Steward Nominations

The Chief Shop Steward nominations have closed as of September 4. Ballots for those who have a challenger went out to the bases, and ballots were due back no later than September 25. The results for Chief Shop Steward will be announced in mid-October. If you forgot to put in a nomination for Shop Steward by the cut-off date of August 28, you can still submit a nomination to me via email, on the Union website, or drop off a nomination form to me in person. Please note that once the revised Shop Steward list is posted, nominations will be closed. Nominations received after the list is released will be added to the Spring Nomination list. Thank you all for your willingness to serve as a Shop Steward, you all do great work, and we all appreciate all that you do!

## Other News, the STANDARD is Coming BACK!

After tireless work, zoom meetings, in person meetings on my personal time in New Jersey, I worked with BBS, our volunteer benefits partner, and we were able to get the Standard back! I heard our membership loud and clear and went to work on this with BBS to see what they could do to get the most commonly asked for item back, the 3-month on the job injury monetary benefit. There will also be an increase to Initial Doctors visit benefit, and the cost for the annual premium will be lower than Unum, while offering more! There's still the option of staying on Unum if an individual wishes, more details to come on how to do this.

In solidarity!
