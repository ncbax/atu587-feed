## Follow Up

### Continuing Education Orca

The Union points out that this is not collaborative process because nobody's in the room from Operations, even though this is the Operators LMRC

Metro put out a turn-by-turn updates on the maps from about a week ago. Metro claims "Turn-by-Turn remains active on vehicles equipped with CoPilot 2 and CoPilot 3, and we have not observed the same OBS issues on those vehicles since the pilot began on May 28. The ongoing issue is isolated to vehicles equipped with CoPilot 1 when Turn-by-Turn is enabled. On those vehicles, we have observed intermittent off-route status, DDU clock jumps, delayed route-position recovery, stop announcement delays, and interior sign mismatches. These symptoms appear to occur when the CoPilot 1 becomes overloaded while processing the added Turn-by-Turn workload, including GPS position updates, map matching, route-position tracking, distance-to-maneuver calculations, and DDU map/navigation rendering. That being said, this is not ready for beginning of the Fall service change." The Union notes that the issues Metro believes are confined to Co-Pilot 1 happen continually on Co-Pilot 2 equipped coaches. Follow up next month
