IMPORTANT NOTICE FOR UNUM CURRENT POLICYHOLDERS

Please read for important information concerning your Short-Term Disability policy

Current members who are Unum Sort-Term Disability policyholders will no longer have their benefits payroll deducted effective 1/1/2027. The Standard will be the new Short Term Disability provider effective 1/1/2027. You must meet with a BBS Benefit Solutions counselor during Open Enrollment to elect your coverage through The Standard to retain your benefit on payroll deduction. Unum policies can be retained on direct pay.
