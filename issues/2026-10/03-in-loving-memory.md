We were saddened to learn of the passing of:

It is with great sadness that we announce the passing of retired Transit Equipment Operator and 50-year member, **Samuel J. Gamble**. Samuel started his Metro career in 1973 and retired in 1998. Samuel is survived by his Wife, Roberta Gamble, and will be deeply missed by his extended loving family and coworkers. Graveside Service has passed.

We are saddened to announce the passing of retired Transit Operator, **William Quade**. William started his Metro journey as a Part-time transit operator in 1991, then promoted to full-time in 1996. In 2011, Willam retired from his transit operator career. William is survived by his wife, Kazzandra Quade. William will be deeply missed by all that had the pleasure of knowing him. There are memorial services, please use the link though the funeral home for more information.

We regret to announce the passing of retired South Base Mechanic, **Stephen Soltesz**. Stephen came on board in 2015 and worked as a mechanic until his retirement until 2022. Stephen is survived by his wife, Andrea Soltesz. Stephen will be missed by his loving family and friends. Services have not been announced.

*Please place these members and their families in your thoughts and hearts*
