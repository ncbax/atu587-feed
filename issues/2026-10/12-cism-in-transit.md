Support starts with us…Critical incidents affect individuals, teams, families, and entire workplaces. A well-prepared peer support network can help ensure that no member of ATU Local 587 must navigate a difficult event alone. Instructor Cherry D. Gilbert, CCISM designed this training for transit and rail professionals to strengthen peer-to-peer support and help build a healthier, more resilient workplace.

- Introduction to Critical Incident Stress Management (CISM)
- The role, purpose, and limits of peer support
- Recognizing signs of stress, trauma, and burnout
- Active listening and supportive communication skills
- Building a sustainable peer-support culture within the workplace
- Peer Support following critical incidents

All participants will receive an ICISF Certificate of Completion. Two-Day Course per subject: Assisting Individuals in Crisis and Group Crisis Intervention General CEUs from University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC) Department of Emergency Health Services.
