In the month of August, we received 116 new active members, 42 active members were deducted, for a net gain of 74 active members over last month's total is 4,950. Our total active membership is now **5,024, an all-time high for Local 587!** A year ago, our active membership was 4,694 for a net gain of 330 in the last 12 months. Three years ago, our active membership was 4,146 for a net gain of 878 members in the last 3 years. Our Retiree Membership is now 843, up 2 from last month.
